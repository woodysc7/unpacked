About Guigang

Guigang, located in the southern Guangxi province of China, offers a delightful blend of traditional culture and modern city life. Wander through vibrant street markets where the fragrant smells of street food waft through the air, or visit the stunning Longtan Park for a peaceful retreat among blossoming flowers and tranquil ponds. The locals are incredibly friendly and always eager to share their rich heritage with visitors. Don't miss trying the local delicacy, Guigang rice noodles, a true culinary delight that will leave your taste buds begging for more. So pack your bags and immerse yourself in the dynamic energy and warm hospitality of Guigang - a hidden gem waiting to be discovered!
