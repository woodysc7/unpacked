About Abidjan

Discover Abidjan, a vibrant metropolis that pulsates with energy and charm. From the bustling markets of Treichville to the serene beaches of Assinie, this city has something for everyone. Indulge in the local delicacies of attiéké and grilled fish, and groove to the infectious rhythms of coupé-décalé music. Immerse yourself in the rich culture and history of Ivory Coast at the National Museum or take a relaxing boat trip along the Ébrié Lagoon. With its fusion of modern skyscrapers and traditional street markets, Abidjan is a melting pot of experiences that will leave you captivated and craving more.
