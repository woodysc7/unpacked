About Niigata

Nestled along the coast of the Sea of Japan, Niigata offers a delightful blend of natural beauty and cultural treasures. Known for its world-class sake breweries, visitors can indulge in sake tasting tours while exploring the picturesque landscapes of terraced rice fields and towering mountains. Art enthusiasts will revel in the Echigo-Tsumari Art Field, a sprawling outdoor art gallery featuring installations by international artists. With its vibrant festivals, delectable seafood, and warm hospitality, Niigata welcomes travelers to experience the essence of Japan in a unique and unforgettable way.
