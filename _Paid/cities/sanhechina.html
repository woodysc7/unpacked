About Sanhe

Sanhe, China, is a charming hidden gem waiting to be discovered by adventurous travelers. This ancient water town, located just a short drive from bustling Beijing, offers a peaceful escape from the urban hustle and bustle. Wander through narrow alleys lined with traditional courtyard homes, indulge in delicious local street food, and take a leisurely boat ride along the tranquil canals. Sanhe's rich history and picturesque scenery make it a perfect off-the-beaten-path destination for those seeking a taste of authentic Chinese culture without the crowds.
