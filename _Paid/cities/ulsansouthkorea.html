About Ulsan

Ulsan, South Korea, a vibrant coastal city just waiting to be explored! From the stunning beaches of Ilsan and the towering peaks of Ganjeolgot to the bustling markets of Taehwa-dong, there's something for everyone in this dynamic destination. Dive into the local culture with a visit to the Ulsan Jangsaengpo Whale Cultural Village and indulge in the freshest seafood at the bustling fish markets. Whether you're a nature lover, history buff, or foodie, Ulsan offers a perfect mix of adventure and relaxation that will leave you craving more of South Korea's hidden gem.
