About Heze

Heze, China is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered! Known as the "City of Peonies," this charming destination boasts stunning peony gardens that will leave you in awe. Embrace the local culture by trying traditional folk art performances, indulging in delicious local cuisine, and exploring the lively night markets. Heze is the perfect blend of vibrant city life and peaceful countryside, offering travelers a unique and unforgettable experience in the heart of Shandong province. Get ready to be enchanted by the beauty and warmth of Heze!
