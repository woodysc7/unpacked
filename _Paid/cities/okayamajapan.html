About Okayama

Nestled between the majestic Seto Inland Sea and the lush green mountains, Okayama is a gem waiting to be discovered in Japan. Known as the "Land of Sunshine," this charming city boasts historical treasures like the iconic Korakuen Garden and the majestic Okayama Castle. Stroll through the quaint streets of the Kurashiki Bikan Historical Quarter, lined with picturesque white-walled buildings and weeping willows, or indulge in some retail therapy at the trendy shopping arcades. And don't forget to savor a bowl of steaming hot Okayama-style udon noodles, a culinary delight that will tantalize your taste buds. Okayama is the perfect blend of culture, history, and natural beauty, offering a truly authentic Japanese experience that will leave you enchanted and craving for more.
