About Pizhou

Nestled in the heart of Jiangsu province, Pizhou is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered by adventurous travelers seeking an authentic taste of rural Chinese life. Known for its picturesque countryside of golden wheat fields and tranquil lotus ponds, Pizhou offers a serene escape from the bustling city life. Wander through ancient villages where time seems to stand still, sample delicious local delicacies like fresh lotus root and crispy Pizhou pancakes, and unwind in the soothing hot springs that dot the landscape. Whether you're exploring historic temples, biking along peaceful rivers, or simply soaking in the natural beauty of this charming town, Pizhou promises a unique and unforgettable travel experience off the beaten path.
