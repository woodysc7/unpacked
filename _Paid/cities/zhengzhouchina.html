About Zhengzhou

Experience the bustling energy of modern China in Zhengzhou, where ancient traditions blend seamlessly with contemporary city life. Marvel at the Shaolin Monks' kung fu skills, sample mouthwatering street food delicacies at Erqi Square, and wander through the historic Zhengzhou Ancient City Ruins to feel the city's rich cultural heritage come to life. With its vibrant mix of old and new, Zhengzhou offers travelers a dynamic and unforgettable journey through the heart of Henan province.
