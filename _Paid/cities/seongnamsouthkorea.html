About Seongnam

Seongnam, just a short hop away from bustling Seoul, offers a delightful blend of traditional Korean charm and modern city vibes. Stroll through the picturesque Tancheon River Park, where locals practice tai chi at sunrise or enjoy a leisurely picnic under swaying willow trees. Don't miss the chance to explore the vibrant Pangyo Techno Valley, a hub of innovation and sleek architecture that will make you feel like you've stepped into the future. Whether you're savoring street food in Moran Market or unwinding in a traditional jjimjilbang spa, Seongnam promises a memorable and refreshing escape from the urban hustle.
