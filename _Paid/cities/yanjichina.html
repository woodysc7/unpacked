About Yanji

Yanji, a charming city tucked away in China's northeastern province of Jilin, offers a unique blend of Korean and Chinese cultures, making for a fascinating and dynamic travel experience. From strolling through bustling markets filled with delectable Korean street food to exploring tranquil parks and historic sites, Yanji has something for every traveler. Don't miss the chance to indulge in a traditional Korean barbecue feast or embark on a scenic hike along the picturesque Changbai Mountain range. With its warm hospitality, rich history, and delicious cuisine, Yanji is a hidden gem just waiting to be discovered by intrepid adventurers seeking an off-the-beaten-path destination.
