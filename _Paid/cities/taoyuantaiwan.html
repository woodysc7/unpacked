About Taoyuan

Taoyuan in Taiwan is a mesmerizing blend of tradition and modernity, where bustling night markets meet serene temples. Sample the delectable street food offerings at Zhongli Night Market, before exploring the stunning Cihu Mausoleum to pay your respects to the late President Chiang Kai-shek. Stroll through the enchanting Daxi Old Street to marvel at quaint tea houses and historical architecture, then wrap up your day with a scenic hike at Cihu Scenic Area to soak in panoramic views of the vibrant cityscape. Taoyuan is a city that promises an unforgettable cultural experience at every turn.
