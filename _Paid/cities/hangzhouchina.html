About Hangzhou

Hangzhou, often described as a "paradise on earth," captivates visitors with its stunning West Lake, surrounded by lush green hills and charming pagodas. Lose yourself in the tranquil beauty of tea plantations at Longjing Village, where you can taste the finest Dragon Well tea. Explore the historic Hefang Street for local snacks and artisanal crafts, or marvel at the grandeur of Lingyin Temple, one of China's oldest Buddhist temples. Don't miss the chance to savor the city's renowned cuisine, like savory beggar's chicken and delicate lotus root soup. Hangzhou effortlessly blends ancient tradition with modern innovation, offering a dynamic and unforgettable travel experience.
