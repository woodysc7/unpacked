About Rostov-on-Don

Rostov-on-Don, a bustling city on the Don River in southern Russia, offers a unique blend of historical charm and vibrant modern energy. Wander through the picturesque streets lined with colorful buildings, soak up the lively atmosphere at local markets, and indulge in delicious traditional Russian cuisine at cozy cafes. Marvel at the ornate architecture of the Cathedral of the Nativity of the Blessed Virgin Mary and immerse yourself in the city's rich cultural heritage at the Rostov Regional Museum of Fine Arts. From savoring borscht in a local eatery to strolling along the scenic riverfront promenade, Rostov-on-Don promises an unforgettable experience filled with warmth, authenticity, and Russian soul.
