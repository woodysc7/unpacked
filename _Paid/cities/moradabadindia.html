About Moradabad

Moradabad, a vibrant city in northern India, invites you to immerse yourself in its rich culture and traditions. Known as the "Brass City" for its exquisite metalwork, Moradabad boasts bustling markets filled with intricate handicrafts and colorful textiles. Indulge your taste buds with local delicacies like kebabs and biryani, and experience the warmth of genuine Indian hospitality. Explore the historic monuments, charming temples, and picturesque gardens that dot the city, offering a glimpse into its fascinating past. Moradabad is a hidden gem just waiting to be discovered, promising a delightful blend of old-world charm and modern-day allure.
