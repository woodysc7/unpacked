About Omsk

Nestled along the Irtysh River in western Siberia, Omsk is a city with a rich history and a vibrant energy that will surprise you. Once a bustling trading post on the famous Siberian Route, today Omsk offers a unique blend of Soviet architecture, modern cafes, and a lively arts scene. Take a stroll down Lenin Street to admire the charming wooden houses or immerse yourself in the local culture at the Omsk State Drama Theater. Whether you're sipping on a steaming cup of Siberian tea or exploring the fascinating history at the Omsk Fortress, this city will captivate you with its authentic Russian charm and warm hospitality.
