About Zhenjiang Jiangsu

Nestled along the majestic Yangtze River, Zhenjiang, Jiangsu offers a perfect blend of ancient charm and modern vibrancy. From the serene beauty of Jinshan Park to the bustling streets of Nanshan Commercial District, this enchanting city caters to every traveler's taste. Delight your senses with local delicacies like sesame oil noodles and famous vinegar, or stroll along the historic Grand Canal, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Whether you seek cultural immersion, culinary adventures, or simply breathtaking scenery, Zhenjiang will captivate you with its rich history and warm hospitality.
