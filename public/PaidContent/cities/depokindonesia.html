About Depok

Nestled just south of bustling Jakarta, Depok offers a refreshing mix of modernity and traditional charm. Explore vibrant markets overflowing with fresh produce and local delicacies, or escape the urban buzz in one of the city's serene parks and gardens. From vibrant street art to delicious street food, Depok is a cultural melting pot waiting to be discovered. Soak up the warmth of Javanese hospitality as you wander through the city's lively streets, and don't forget to snap a selfie in front of the iconic Depok Grand Mosque. Join the locals in savoring nasi goreng at a humble warung or treat yourself to a traditional dance performance. Whether you're seeking adventure or simply looking to unwind, Depok promises an unforgettable Indonesian experience.
