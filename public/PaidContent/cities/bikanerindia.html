About Bikaner

Bikaner, a vibrant city in the heart of Rajasthan, is a delightful blend of history, culture, and mouthwatering cuisine. Explore the majestic Junagarh Fort, marvel at the intricate architecture of the havelis, and immerse yourself in the bustling bazaars filled with colorful textiles and traditional Rajasthani handicrafts. Don't miss the opportunity to savor the famous Bikaneri bhujia and indulge in the delectable sweets that will leave your taste buds tingling. With its warm hospitality and rich heritage, Bikaner is sure to captivate your senses and leave you craving for more of its charm.
