About Weifang

Nestled in the heart of Shandong province, Weifang is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered by intrepid travelers seeking authentic Chinese culture. Known as the "Kite Capital of the World," this vibrant city offers a unique blend of ancient traditions and modern innovations. Visitors can explore the colorful kite museum, wander through bustling street markets brimming with local delicacies, or marvel at the intricate architecture of the historic Fangong Pavilion. With its warm hospitality and fascinating history, Weifang is a delightful destination for those looking to experience the true essence of China.
