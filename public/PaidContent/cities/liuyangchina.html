About Liuyang

Nestled in the heart of Hunan province, Liuyang is not just a destination known for its vibrant fireworks industry, but also a hidden gem waiting to be discovered. Travelers will be enchanted by the charming ancient town with its narrow alleyways, historic temples, and bustling markets. From savory local street food to traditional tea houses and picturesque bamboo forests, Liuyang offers a rich tapestry of experiences that will leave visitors spellbound. Soak in the authentic culture, witness the artistry of fireworks displays, and immerse yourself in the welcoming warmth of the locals. Liuyang is a delightful blend of tradition and modernity, promising a memorable adventure for all who venture off the beaten path.
