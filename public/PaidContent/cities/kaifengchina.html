About Kaifeng

Kaifeng, a hidden gem in central China, effortlessly blends ancient history with modern charm. Stroll through the bustling streets lined with traditional architecture, vibrant markets, and mouthwatering street food stalls offering delicious local delicacies. Don't miss the chance to explore the fascinating Dragon Pavilion, wander the picturesque Qingming Riverside Landscape Garden, and immerse yourself in the rich history of the Song Dynasty at the Kaifeng City Museum. With its welcoming locals and authentic cultural experiences, Kaifeng is a must-visit destination for travelers seeking a unique and unforgettable adventure in China.
