About Yingkou

Yingkou, China, is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered! This coastal city is a perfect blend of tradition and modernity, where ancient temples stand tall next to sleek skyscrapers. Stroll down the charming streets filled with local vendors selling delicious street food like crispy jianbing and savory lamb skewers. Explore the stunning beaches, such as Xianrendao Beach, where you can relax on the soft sand or try your hand at water sports. Don't miss the breathtaking views from Mount Xilv, offering a panoramic vista of the city and the Bohai Sea. Yingkou is a vibrant destination that promises an unforgettable adventure!
