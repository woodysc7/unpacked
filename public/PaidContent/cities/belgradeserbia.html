About Belgrade

Belgrade, the vibrant capital of Serbia, effortlessly blends history with a buzzing contemporary energy. Dive into the city's complex past at the imposing Belgrade Fortress before strolling down Knez Mihailova Street for some serious retail therapy. Indulge in hearty traditional dishes like cevapi and sarma at lively kafanas, or sip on rakija in the trendy Savamala district. Belgrade's diverse nightlife scene is legendary, with floating clubs along the Danube River and underground venues that keep the party going until dawn. Get ready for an unforgettable mix of Balkan charm and urban cool in this dynamic city.
