About Wuxi

Nestled on the banks of Lake Tai, Wuxi is a vibrant blend of ancient charm and modern allure. Stroll along the picturesque canals lined with traditional stone bridges, or marvel at the futuristic skyline featuring towering skyscrapers. Indulge in the local delicacies at bustling night markets, savoring dishes like savory xiaolongbao and crispy sesame cakes. Don't miss a boat ride to the serene Turtle Head Isle, where lush gardens and historic temples offer a peaceful retreat from the city buzz. In Wuxi, history and innovation harmonize to create a truly captivating destination that promises endless surprises at every turn.
