About Santiago

Santiago, Chile, at first glance may seem like just a bustling metropolis surrounded by the Andes mountains, but delve deeper and you'll discover its vibrant soul. From the historic streets of Barrio Lastarria lined with charming cafes and art galleries, to the bustling markets of La Vega Central where you can indulge in local produce and street food, Santiago offers a perfect blend of tradition and modernity. Whether you're sipping on a pisco sour at a rooftop bar overlooking the cityscape or hiking up Cerro San Cristobal for panoramic views, Santiago's irresistible energy is sure to captivate you from the moment you arrive.
