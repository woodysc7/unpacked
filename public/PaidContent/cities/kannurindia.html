About Kannur

Kannur, a charming coastal town in Kerala, India, is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered. Known for its pristine beaches, rich history, and vibrant cultural heritage, Kannur offers a perfect blend of relaxation and adventure. Dive into the local art scene with a visit to Muzhappilangad Beach, where traditional Theyyam performances mesmerize spectators. Explore the historic St. Angelo's Fort, a 16th-century Portuguese stronghold overlooking the Arabian Sea. Indulge in mouthwatering Malabar cuisine at local eateries, and don't miss the chance to witness the unique ritual of Theyyam dance ceremonies. Whether you're a history buff, a beach lover, or a foodie, Kannur has something to offer every type of traveler.
