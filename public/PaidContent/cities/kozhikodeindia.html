About Kozhikode

Nestled on the Malabar Coast, Kozhikode in India is a delightful blend of history, culture, and culinary wonders. Take a leisurely stroll along the iconic Kozhikode Beach, soak in the vibrant atmosphere of the bustling markets, and don't miss indulging in the famous Malabar biryani for a true taste of the region. Uncover the rich maritime history at the Pazhassiraja Museum, marvel at the intricate architecture of the Tali Temple, and make sure to witness the captivating sunset on Beypore Beach. With its warm hospitality and flavors that will tantalize your taste buds, Kozhikode promises an unforgettable and authentic Indian experience.
