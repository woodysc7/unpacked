About Miluo

Nestled on the banks of the stunning Miluo River in China, the charming town of Miluo effortlessly blends natural beauty with rich history. Explore the picturesque Old Street lined with traditional tea houses and ancient architecture, or cruise along the tranquil river surrounded by breathtaking scenery. Don't miss the chance to taste the local delicacies, from savory stinky tofu to sweet osmanthus cakes. With its laid-back vibe and warm hospitality, Miluo is the perfect destination for travelers seeking a peaceful escape immersed in authentic Chinese culture.
