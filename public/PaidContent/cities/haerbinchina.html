About Haerbin

Harbin, China, is a winter wonderland waiting to be explored! With its stunning ice sculptures, vibrant ice lantern festivals, and world-renowned ice and snow architecture, this city is a paradise for those seeking a unique and magical experience. Don't miss out on trying the local specialty, Harbin-style ice cream, which is a true delight in the chilly weather. Whether you're wandering through the frozen sculptures at the Harbin Ice and Snow World or savoring a hearty bowl of hotpot in one of the cozy restaurants, Haerbin is sure to leave you spellbound with its enchanting winter charm.
