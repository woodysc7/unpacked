About Changshu

Changshu, a charming gem tucked away in eastern China, boasts a rich tapestry of tradition and modernity waiting to be explored. Wander through ancient water towns and lush silk-filled gardens that whisper tales of dynasties past, before diving headfirst into a bustling city center teeming with vibrant markets and delectable street food stalls. Immerse yourself in the bustling energy of this dynamic city, where centuries-old pagodas stand tall against a backdrop of sleek skyscrapers. Changshu's fascinating juxtapositions will captivate your senses and leave you yearning for more.
