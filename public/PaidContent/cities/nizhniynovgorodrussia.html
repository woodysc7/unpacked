About Nizhniy Novgorod

Nestled on the confluence of the Oka and Volga rivers, Nizhniy Novgorod is a charming blend of old-world charm and modern dynamism. Home to a spectacular hilltop kremlin offering panoramic views of the city, a vibrant arts and culture scene, and a bustling food market filled with delectable Russian treats, this city seamlessly combines history and innovation. Whether you're exploring the colorful wooden houses of Old Town, taking a leisurely stroll along the riverbanks, or immersing yourself in the lively atmosphere of the local bars and cafes, Nizhniy Novgorod invites you to discover its unique spirit and warm hospitality in every corner.
