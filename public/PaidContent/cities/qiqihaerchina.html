About Qiqihaer

Qiqihaer, a vibrant city in northeastern China, offers a delightful mix of modern amenities and rich cultural heritage. From the majestic Zhalong Nature Reserve teeming with diverse bird species to the charming Zhonghua Square boasting Soviet-style architecture, there's something for every type of traveler. Don't miss the chance to indulge in delicious local cuisine like hearty lamb skewers and savory dumplings, or explore the bustling night markets filled with unique treasures. Whether you're seeking outdoor adventures, historical insights, or simply a taste of authentic Chinese life, Qiqihaer promises a memorable and immersive experience that will leave you enchanted and wanting more.
