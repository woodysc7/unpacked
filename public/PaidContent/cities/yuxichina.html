About Yuxi

Yuxi, a hidden gem in China's Yunnan province, offers a delightful mix of cultural experiences and natural beauty. Wander through charming ancient towns like Jiangchuan and Xinping, where cobblestone streets lead to traditional tea houses and bustling markets. Don't miss the mesmerizing landscapes of Fuxian Lake, one of the clearest lakes in China, perfect for boating and relaxation. Indulge in local delicacies like Yiliang roast duck and Xuanwei ham, and immerse yourself in the warm hospitality of the locals. Yuxi may not be on every traveler's radar, but those who discover its charm will be rewarded with unforgettable memories.
