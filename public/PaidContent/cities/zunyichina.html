About Zunyi

Nestled in the heart of Guizhou province, Zunyi beckons travelers with its captivating mix of ancient charm and modern vibrancy. History buffs will revel in exploring the famed Zunyi Conference site, a pivotal moment in China's revolutionary past. Culinary enthusiasts can delight in local delicacies like spicy hot pot and sour fish soup, while nature lovers can find solace in the picturesque parks and verdant mountains surrounding the city. With its welcoming locals, bustling markets, and rich cultural heritage, Zunyi promises an unforgettable adventure for those seeking an authentic taste of China off the beaten path.
