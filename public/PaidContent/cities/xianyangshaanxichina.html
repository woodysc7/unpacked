About Xianyang Shaanxi

Immerse yourself in ancient history and vibrant culture in Xianyang, Shaanxi, China. From the awe-inspiring Terracotta Army to the grandeur of the Han and Tang dynasties, this city is a treasure trove of archaeological wonders. Stroll through bustling markets, savor local delicacies like biang biang noodles, and witness the harmonious blend of old and new in this dynamic city. With its rich history and warm hospitality, Xianyang is a must-visit destination for history buffs and adventurers alike.
