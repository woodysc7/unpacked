About Suwon

Suwon, South Korea is a delightful blend of ancient traditions and modern innovations, all wrapped up in a bustling city bursting with energy. Walk along the iconic Hwaseong Fortress, a UNESCO World Heritage site, and immerse yourself in the rich history of this dynamic city. Indulge in mouthwatering Korean BBQ at lively street markets, sip on traditional makgeolli at cozy cafes, and experience a thrilling taste of local life. Suwon's vibrant charm and welcoming locals make it a must-visit destination for any traveler looking to dive into the heart of South Korea's culture and spirit.
