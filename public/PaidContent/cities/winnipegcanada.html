About Winnipeg

Nestled within the heart of the Canadian prairies, Winnipeg is a vibrant city that effortlessly blends history with modernity. From the fascinating exhibits at the Canadian Museum for Human Rights to the lively energy of The Forks, where the Assiniboine and Red Rivers meet, there is no shortage of things to see and do. Explore the charming neighborhoods like The Exchange District, known for its historic architecture and trendy boutiques, or immerse yourself in the local culture at one of the many festivals that take place throughout the year. With its friendly locals, delicious cuisine (don't miss trying a classic Winnipeg goldeye sandwich), and an abundance of green spaces, Winnipeg is a hidden gem just waiting to be discovered.
