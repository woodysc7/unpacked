About Guiping

Nestled in the picturesque Guangxi region of China, Guiping is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered by curious travelers. With its lush green landscapes, serene rivers, and charming traditional villages, Guiping offers a peaceful escape from the hustle and bustle of city life. Explore the stunning Baojing Palace overlooking the majestic Qian River, or wander through the historic streets lined with ancient temples and teahouses. Don't miss the chance to savor the local delicacies, from spicy hotpot to savory rice noodles. Whether you're seeking adventure or simply a tranquil retreat, Guiping promises an unforgettable experience off the beaten path.
