About Kagoshima

Nestled at the southern tip of Japan, Kagoshima is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered. With its stunning views of Sakurajima volcano looming in the distance, this city effortlessly combines natural beauty with urban charm. Indulge in Kagoshima's culinary delights, from savory kurobuta pork to the famous sweet potato shochu. Immerse yourself in the local culture by visiting historic sites like Senganen Garden and exploring the bustling Tenmonkan shopping arcade. Whether you're soaking in an onsen with a view or watching a traditional Kagura dance performance, Kagoshima promises a unique and unforgettable adventure that will leave you longing to return.
