About Ardabil

Nestled amidst the stunning landscapes of northern Iran, Ardabil invites travelers to uncover its hidden gems and rich heritage. Known for its therapeutic hot springs, visitors can soak in relaxation while exploring the tranquil gardens of Sheikh Safi al-Din Khanegah and marveling at the intricate tile work of the Jameh Mosque. Don't miss the chance to savor local delicacies like the savory mirza ghasemi or indulge in the sweet delight of tas kabob. With friendly locals eager to share their stories and breathtaking natural beauty at every turn, Ardabil promises a memorable adventure off the beaten path.
