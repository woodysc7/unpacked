About Kitwe

Kitwe, Zambia is a vibrant city that pulsates with energy and warmth, offering a unique blend of traditional African culture and modern amenities. Get lost in the bustling markets, where the colorful fabrics and intriguing crafts beckon to be explored. Indulge in the mouth-watering local cuisine, like nshima and dried fish, for a true taste of Zambia. For nature lovers, a visit to the nearby Chimfunshi Wildlife Orphanage provides a heartwarming experience with rescued chimpanzees. Kitwe is a city that will captivate your senses and leave you with unforgettable memories of Zambia's rich tapestry of sights, sounds, and flavors.
