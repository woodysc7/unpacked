About Chengde

Nestled in the misty mountains of northeastern China, Chengde is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered by adventurous travelers seeking a break from the hustle and bustle of city life. Home to the glorious Chengde Mountain Resort, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, this imperial playground captures the essence of tranquility and grandeur. Stroll through the vast gardens, marvel at the intricate temples, and immerse yourself in the rich history of this once imperial retreat. Whether you're seeking cultural enlightenment or simply a peaceful escape, Chengde promises a journey filled with surprises and serenity amidst its breathtaking natural beauty.
