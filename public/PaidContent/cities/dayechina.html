About Daye

Nestled in the heart of Hubei province, Daye is a delightful blend of ancient Chinese history and modern urban charm. Explore the fascinating remnants of the city's rich mining heritage at the picturesque iron ore mines, then wander through the bustling streets filled with mouthwatering local delicacies and vibrant markets. Don't miss the chance to relax in the tranquil natural beauty of Piyun Mountain, where breathtaking views and peaceful serenity await. Whether you're a history buff, foodie, or nature lover, Daye promises an unforgettable adventure filled with surprises around every corner.
