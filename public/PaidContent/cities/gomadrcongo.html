About Goma

Goma is a city of stark contrasts, where the breathtaking beauty of Lake Kivu meets the striking backdrop of the Nyiragongo volcano. The bustling markets, vibrant street life, and warm hospitality of the locals create a dynamic energy that is both chaotic and captivating. While the remnants of past conflicts are visible, Goma's resilience and spirit shine through in its colorful street art and lively music scene. Whether hiking up the volcano for a once-in-a-lifetime lava lake experience or unwinding on the shores of Lake Kivu with freshly grilled fish, Goma offers a unique and unforgettable adventure for intrepid travelers.
