About Riga

Welcome to Riga, a captivating city where medieval charm meets vibrant nightlife! Wander through the cobblestone streets of the UNESCO-listed Old Town, admiring the stunning architecture of churches and colorful Art Nouveau buildings. Indulge in mouthwatering traditional Latvian dishes at local restaurants, and don't miss the chance to try the famous Black Balsam liqueur. Experience the bustling energy of the Central Market, housed in Zeppelin hangars, or relax in one of the many scenic parks along the Daugava River. Whether you're a history buff, foodie, or party animal, Riga has something for everyone to enjoy!
