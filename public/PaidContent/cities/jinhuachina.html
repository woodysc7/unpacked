About Jinhua

Nestled among picturesque mountains and lush greenery, Jinhua in China's Zhejiang province is a delightful mix of ancient charm and modern vibrancy. Stroll through the historic streets of Wucheng district, lined with traditional Huizhou architecture and bustling markets selling everything from local delicacies to handicrafts. Don't miss a visit to the serene Yiwu Wetland Park, offering a peaceful retreat amidst nature. For a taste of cultural richness, explore the Jinhua Museum and marvel at its impressive collection of artifacts. With its captivating blend of heritage and contemporary allure, Jinhua promises a memorable journey for every traveler.
