About Belgaum

Belgaum, a hidden gem nestled in the heart of Karnataka, offers a delightful mix of history, nature, and culture waiting to be explored. Immerse yourself in the architectural wonders of Belgaum Fort, built by the Marathas in the 13th century, or unwind amidst the lush greenery of Jamboti Hills. Indulge in the vibrant local cuisine bursting with flavors and spices, and don't miss the chance to witness the traditional folk performances that bring this charming city to life. Belgaum may not be on every traveler's radar, but those in the know will discover a truly unforgettable experience off the beaten path.
