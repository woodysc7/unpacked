About Tonghua

Discover the hidden gem of Tonghua, China, where tradition meets modernity in a captivating blend. Wander through bustling night markets filled with tantalizing street food, or immerse yourself in the serenity of the Changbai Mountain foothills. Sample the renowned local delicacy, Bingfen jelly, or take a leisurely boat ride along the tranquil Fengman Reservoir. Whether you're seeking vibrant city life or peaceful natural beauty, Tonghua offers a dynamic tapestry of experiences waiting to be explored.
