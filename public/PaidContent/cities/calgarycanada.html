About Calgary

Nestled in the heart of the Canadian Rockies, Calgary is a vibrant city bursting with western charm and outdoor adventures. From exploring the iconic Calgary Tower and taking a stroll through the historic Stephen Avenue Walk to experiencing the adrenaline rush of the annual Calgary Stampede, there is no shortage of excitement in this lively metropolis. Outdoor enthusiasts will revel in the expansive network of hiking and biking trails, while foodies can indulge in the diverse culinary scene offering everything from sizzling steakhouses to trendy food markets. So, whether you're seeking thrills on the ski slopes or looking to unwind in a cozy café, Calgary has something for everyone to enjoy.
