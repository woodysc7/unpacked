About Siping

Nestled in the heart of Jilin province, Siping offers a perfect blend of traditional charm and modern vibrancy. From exploring the stunning temples and pagodas dotting the cityscape to immersing yourself in the bustle of the local markets filled with exotic delicacies, Siping is a sensory delight waiting to be discovered. Take a leisurely stroll along the picturesque Xiwanzi Lake, or challenge your taste buds with the fiery local cuisine. With its friendly locals and unique cultural attractions, Siping is a hidden gem just waiting to be unearthed by intrepid travelers seeking an authentic Chinese experience.
