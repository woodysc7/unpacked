About Suqian

Nestled in the heart of Jiangsu province, Suqian is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered by intrepid travelers seeking an authentic Chinese experience. This vibrant city seamlessly blends ancient traditions with modern innovation, offering a unique glimpse into the rich cultural tapestry of China. From wandering through serene traditional gardens to indulging in mouthwatering local delicacies at bustling night markets, Suqian captures the essence of a city brimming with charm and charisma. Whether you're exploring historical landmarks like the stunning Yunlong Lake or simply getting lost in the labyrinthine alleyways of the old town, Suqian is a destination that promises a truly unforgettable adventure off the beaten path.
