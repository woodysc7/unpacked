About Algiers

Algiers, the vibrant capital of Algeria, is a city that effortlessly blends tradition with modernity. Get lost in the maze-like streets of the ancient Casbah, a UNESCO World Heritage site, where you'll stumble upon hidden mosques, bustling markets, and stunning Ottoman architecture. Feast on delectable couscous dishes and sip on sweet mint tea at charming cafes while soaking in the city's intoxicating energy. For a dose of history, visit the Bardo Museum and explore its impressive collection of Roman artifacts. With its stunning Mediterranean coastline, rich culture, and warm hospitality, Algiers is a captivating destination that will leave you wanting more.
