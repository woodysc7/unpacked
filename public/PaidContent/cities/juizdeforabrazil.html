About Juiz De Fora

Juiz De Fora, Brazil, may not be on everyone's radar, but this vibrant city hidden in the hills of Minas Gerais is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered. From its bustling markets offering delicious street food to its historic architecture that tells tales of a bygone era, Juiz De Fora surprises at every turn. Take a scenic train ride through lush mountains, explore the charming old town filled with colorful colonial buildings, or sip on a refreshing caipirinha at a local bar while tapping your feet to the sounds of live samba music. Whether you're a culture vulture, foodie, or nature lover, Juiz De Fora offers a taste of authentic Brazilian charm that will leave you wanting more.
