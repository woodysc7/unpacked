About Pachuca de Soto

Nestled in the heart of central Mexico, Pachuca de Soto is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered by adventurous travelers. This charming city seamlessly blends history with modernity, boasting vibrant murals that grace its streets and a stunning architectural mix of colonial and Art Nouveau buildings. Be sure to sample the local delicacy, pastes, a delicious pastry filled with savory goodness. Dive into the mining history at the Surrealistic Garden of Edward James or explore the majestic Grutas de Tolantongo for a dip in the natural hot springs. Pachuca de Soto offers a unique and authentic Mexican experience that is sure to leave a lasting impression.
