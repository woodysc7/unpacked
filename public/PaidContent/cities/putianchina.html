About Putian

Nestled in the Fujian province of China, Putian is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered. This vibrant city offers a fascinating mix of ancient temples, bustling markets, and serene beaches. Immerse yourself in the local culture by sampling traditional Fujian cuisine, such as the famous Putian Mazu Cake, or explore the picturesque Meizhou Island for stunning ocean views. Whether you're seeking adventure, relaxation, or a taste of authentic Chinese life, Putian has something for every traveler to enjoy.
