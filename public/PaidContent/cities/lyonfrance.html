About Lyon

Nestled in the heart of the Rhône-Alpes region, Lyon effortlessly blends old-world charm with a trendy, vibrant atmosphere. Indulge in a culinary journey through the city's bouchons, savoring traditional Lyonnais cuisine like coq au vin and quenelles. Stroll through the traboules of Vieux Lyon, uncovering hidden passageways and stunning Renaissance architecture at every turn. Take a sunset cruise along the Saône River, admiring the city's illuminated bridges and historic landmarks. From the bustling markets of Les Halles de Lyon to the impressive basilica of Notre-Dame de Fourvière, Lyon invites you to immerse yourself in its rich history and culinary delights, promising a truly unforgettable experience.
