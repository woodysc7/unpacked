About Haimen

Nestled on the banks of the Yangtze River, Haimen effortlessly blends traditional Chinese charm with modern vibrancy. Take a leisurely stroll along the historic Riverside Avenue, lined with ancient temples and lush gardens, or indulge in a culinary adventure at bustling street markets offering local delicacies like steamed buns and fresh seafood. For a dose of culture, visit the Haimen Museum to uncover the city's rich history. Whether you're seeking serenity by the water's edge or craving a taste of authentic Chinese culture, Haimen promises a delightful escape off the beaten path.
