About Shizuoka

Nestled between the stunning Mount Fuji and the refreshing Pacific Ocean, Shizuoka effortlessly blends natural beauty with rich cultural heritage. Embrace the serene tea fields of Uji and the vibrant vibes of Hamamatsu, where the sound of music (literally, with its renowned musical instrument industry) fills the air. Indulge in the freshest seafood at Suruga Bay, savor some matcha ice cream in Shimizu, and rejuvenate in a traditional onsen overlooking the picturesque landscapes. With its warm hospitality and diverse landscapes, Shizuoka promises a delightful adventure for any traveler seeking a perfect mix of relaxation and exploration.
