About Mianyang Sichuan

Nestled in the heart of Sichuan province, Mianyang charms visitors with its unique blend of traditional Chinese culture and modern urban flair. Explore ancient temples alongside bustling night markets, where the tantalizing aroma of spicy hotpot wafts through the air. Don't miss a chance to witness the famous Mianyang Dragon Boat Festival, a vibrant celebration of local folklore and community spirit. Whether you're a foodie seeking culinary adventures or a history buff eager to uncover hidden gems, Mianyang offers a delightful tapestry of experiences that will leave you enchanted and craving more.
