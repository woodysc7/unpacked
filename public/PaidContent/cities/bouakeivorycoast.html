About Bouake

Bouake, Ivory Coast, is a vibrant city ready to charm you with its colorful markets, lively music scene, and warm hospitality. Stroll through the bustling streets filled with the aroma of exotic spices, and indulge in mouthwatering local delicacies such as attiéké and aloko. Immerse yourself in the rich culture by visiting the intriguing Musee des Civilisations de Cote d'Ivoire and taking part in traditional dance performances. For an adventure off the beaten path, explore the nearby scenic waterfalls at Mont Péko National Park. Bouake is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered by intrepid travelers seeking an authentic African experience.
