About Zhoushan

Zhoushan, China, will charm you with its blend of picturesque islands, fresh seafood delights, and vibrant maritime culture. Lose yourself in the labyrinth of ancient streets in Putuoshan, where incense-filled temples beckon you to explore. Indulge in the catch of the day at one of the bustling fishing villages, or hop on a boat to marvel at the iconic sea arches of Shengsi. Whether you're a history buff, a foodie, or a nature enthusiast, Zhoushan offers a unique blend of experiences that will leave you longing for more.
