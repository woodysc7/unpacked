About Kunming

Kunming, known as the "City of Eternal Spring," charms visitors with its year-round pleasant climate, vibrant cultural scene, and delicious cuisine. From strolling through the colorful alleys of the historic Old Town to admiring the stunning stone forest at Shilin, this city offers a perfect blend of tradition and modernity. Don't miss the chance to savor a bowl of steaming hot pot while sipping on some local pu'er tea. Whether you're exploring the bustling markets or hiking the picturesque Western Hills, Kunming promises an unforgettable adventure for every traveler seeking a delightful mix of nature and urban excitement in China.
