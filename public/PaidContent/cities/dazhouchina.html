About Dazhou

Experience the unique blend of modernity and tradition in Dazhou, China. Wander through bustling markets filled with exotic fruits and local delicacies, or explore ancient temples nestled amid high-rise buildings. Indulge in authentic Sichuan cuisine that will ignite your taste buds, and witness spectacular performances showcasing the rich cultural heritage of the region. Whether you seek adventure in the rugged countryside or tranquility in serene parks, Dazhou offers a delightful mix of old and new waiting to be discovered.
