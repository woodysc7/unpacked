About Tianjin

Welcome to Tianjin, a vibrant mix of old-world charm and modern hustle! Stroll along the historic streets lined with colonial-era architecture, sample local delicacies at the bustling food markets, and marvel at the intricate design of the Porcelain House. Experience the city's unique blend of cultures at the Ancient Culture Street, where traditional crafts and performances take you back in time. Don't forget to ride the iconic Tianjin Eye ferris wheel for panoramic views of this dynamic city. Tianjin is a hidden gem waiting to be explored!
