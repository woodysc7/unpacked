About Edmonton

Welcome to Edmonton, where winter is fierce but the hospitality is warmer than a cozy cabin fire! This vibrant city in Alberta's heartland offers a delightful mix of urban charm and natural beauty. Dive into the lively arts scene at the Old Strathcona district, savor some delicious poutine after ice skating at the iconic West Edmonton Mall, and don't miss the breathtaking views from the top of the Edmonton Tower. Whether you're a foodie, outdoor enthusiast, or culture buff, Edmonton has something to keep you entertained and captivated all year round!
