About Nanjing

Nanjing, a city steeped in history and culture, offers a blend of ancient wonders and modern charm. From the majestic Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum standing tall on Purple Mountain to the bustling Confucius Temple area filled with traditional shops and street food vendors, Nanjing captivates visitors with its vibrant energy. Stroll along the ancient city wall at sunset for breathtaking views, then dive into the local culinary scene with a hot bowl of steaming Nanjing salted duck soup. Whether you're exploring the past at the Nanjing Massacre Memorial or marveling at the contemporary architecture of the Zifeng Tower, this dynamic city promises an unforgettable journey through time and tradition.
