About Danyang

Nestled amongst the stunning mountains of Jiangsu Province, Danyang is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered by adventurous travelers. Known for its breathtaking natural scenery, including the majestic Danyang Gorge and picturesque Yuneng Marsh, this charming city offers a perfect blend of outdoor adventure and cultural exploration. Whether you're hiking along the rugged trails, sampling delectable local cuisine, or marveling at ancient temples, Danyang promises an unforgettable journey off the beaten path in the heart of China.
