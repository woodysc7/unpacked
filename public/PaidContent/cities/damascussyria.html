About Damascus

Damascus, the ancient jewel of the Middle East, will transport you back in time with its labyrinthine streets, bustling souks, and iconic Umayyad Mosque. Despite its turbulent past, the city exudes resilience and charm, evident in the warmth of its people and the flavors of its exquisite cuisine. From savoring aromatic teas in historic cafes to getting lost in the sights and sounds of Old Damascus, every corner of this city tells a story waiting to be discovered. Immerse yourself in the rich tapestry of culture, history, and hospitality that defines Damascus, and you'll leave with a piece of its magic forever etched in your heart.
