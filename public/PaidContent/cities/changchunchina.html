About Changchun

Nestled in the heart of Northeast China, Changchun is a lively city that effortlessly blends tradition with modernity. Home to stunning imperial architecture, including the glorious Puppet Emperor's Palace, and vibrant street markets offering a feast for the senses, Changchun promises a delightful cultural adventure. World-class museums, such as the Museum of the Imperial Palace of Manchukuo, provide a fascinating glimpse into the region's history. Nature enthusiasts can escape to Jingyuetan National Forest Park for serene lakeside picnics or winter activities in the snow-covered wonderland. With its warm hospitality and delectable local cuisine, Changchun welcomes travelers to immerse themselves in an authentic Chinese experience.
