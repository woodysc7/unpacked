About Mbeya

Nestled in the stunning Southern Highlands of Tanzania, Mbeya is a delightful gem waiting to be discovered. With its vibrant markets bursting with colorful spices and local handicrafts, this bustling town offers a true taste of authentic Tanzanian culture. Immerse yourself in the lush green hills and cool mountain air as you trek through the surrounding tea plantations, or take a dip in the refreshing waters of Kiwira River. And don't miss the chance to savor some of the most flavorful coffee in the country at the renowned Utengule Coffee Estate. Whether you're a nature enthusiast, a foodie, or a culture seeker, Mbeya has something special for every traveler.
