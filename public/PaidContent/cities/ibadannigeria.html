About Ibadan

Nestled in southwestern Nigeria, Ibadan is a vibrant city that effortlessly blends traditional Yoruba culture with modern influences. Explore the towering ancient city walls, vibrant markets like Bodija, and indulge your taste buds with the spicy flavors of local delicacies at Amala joints. From the breathtaking views atop the iconic Cocoa House to the rhythmic beats of live music at Mokola Roundabout, Ibadan offers a captivating blend of history, culture, and hospitality that will leave you wanting more. Get lost in the bustling streets, soak up the warm sun, and immerse yourself in the rich tapestry of experiences that make Ibadan a hidden gem waiting to be explored.
