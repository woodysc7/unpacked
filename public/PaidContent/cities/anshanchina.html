About Anshan

Nestled in the heart of Liaoning Province, Anshan is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered by intrepid travelers. Known for its rich industrial history, this vibrant city offers a unique blend of ancient culture and modern development. From exploring the old steel mills that once powered the region to savoring delectable local dishes like the famous Guancang roasted chicken, Anshan promises a sensory adventure like no other. Take a leisurely stroll along Qianshan National Park's picturesque trails, marvel at the breathtaking views from the North Peak, or immerse yourself in the tranquility of the Jade Buddha Temple. With its warm and welcoming locals, Anshan invites you to not just visit, but to truly experience its authenticity and charm.
