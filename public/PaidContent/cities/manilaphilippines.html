About Manila

Manila, the vibrant capital of the Philippines, is a bustling metropolis that seamlessly blends colonial history with modern urban flair. From exploring the historic walled city of Intramuros to indulging in the lively street food scene at Mercato Centrale, Manila offers a diverse range of experiences for every traveler. Dive into the chaotic energy of Divisoria market for bargain hunting, or escape to the serene oasis of Rizal Park for a moment of peace amidst the city hustle. With its warm hospitality, mouthwatering cuisine, and infectious energy, Manila is a destination that will surprise, delight, and leave you craving more.
