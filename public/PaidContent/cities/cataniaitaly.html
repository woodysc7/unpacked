About Catania

Get ready to soak in the vibrant energy of Catania, Italy! This bustling city on the east coast of Sicily will captivate you with its mix of ancient history and modern charm. Lose yourself in the chaotic beauty of the famous fish market, La Pescheria, where locals haggle over the freshest catches of the day. Climb up to the top of Mount Etna, Europe's highest active volcano, for panoramic views that will take your breath away. And don't forget to indulge in the city's culinary delights, from arancini to granita, every bite will transport you to a foodie paradise. Catania is a whirlwind of excitement just waiting to be explored!
