About Fushun Liaoning

Fushun in Liaoning, China, is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered by adventurous travelers seeking an authentic Chinese experience off the beaten path. This industrial city's unique charm lies in its juxtaposition of ancient history and modern infrastructure, with the stunning Fushun War Criminals Management Area offering a somber yet important glimpse into the region's past. Explore the majestic Xihu Park with its serene lake and vibrant gardens, then indulge in the local cuisine at bustling street markets where fragrant aromas and colorful dishes entice the senses. Whether you're wandering through ancient temples or marveling at the grandeur of Fushun National Forest Park, this dynamic city offers a fascinating blend of tradition and innovation that will leave you captivated and eager for more.
