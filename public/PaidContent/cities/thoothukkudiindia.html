About Thoothukkudi

Known as the "Pearl City" of India, Thoothukkudi is a coastal gem waiting to be discovered by travelers looking for authentic experiences off the beaten path. From its bustling harbor where fishermen bring in the day's catch to its pristine beaches with inviting turquoise waters, this vibrant city offers a glimpse into traditional South Indian coastal life. Indulge in the freshest seafood dishes, explore the historic temples, or simply relax under the swaying palm trees as you soak in the laid-back atmosphere of Thoothukkudi. With its unique charm and warm hospitality, this hidden seaside town promises to leave a lasting impression on every visitor.
