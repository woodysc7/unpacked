About Samut Prakan

Located just a stone's throw away from the bustling metropolis of Bangkok, Samut Prakan offers a delightful escape into authentic Thai culture without straying too far from the conveniences of the big city. Home to the fascinating Ancient City, where you can wander through replicas of Thailand's most iconic landmarks, this hidden gem also boasts the charming floating market of Bang Nam Pheung, perfect for sampling mouthwatering local street food and shopping for unique souvenirs. Explore the ornate temples, meander through serene mangrove forests, or hop on a boat to cruise along the Chao Phraya River for a laid-back day trip filled with cultural discoveries and untamed beauty.
