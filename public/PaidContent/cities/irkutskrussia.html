About Irkutsk

Nestled on the shores of the stunning Lake Baikal, Irkutsk welcomes travelers with its unique blend of historic charm and natural beauty. Delight in the intricately carved wooden architecture of the city center, sample hearty Siberian cuisine at local eateries, and immerse yourself in the rich cultural heritage at museums and galleries. For the adventurous at heart, Irkutsk serves as a gateway to exploring the wild wonders of the Baikal region. Whether you're cruising on the deepest lake in the world, trekking through pristine forests, or discovering the legends of shamanic traditions, Irkutsk promises an unforgettable journey through the heart of Siberia.
