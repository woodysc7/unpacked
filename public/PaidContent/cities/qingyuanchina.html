About Qingyuan

Nestled in the heart of Guangdong province, Qingyuan in China is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered by adventurous travelers. With its lush landscapes, picturesque rivers, and breathtaking waterfalls, this charming city offers a perfect blend of natural beauty and cultural experiences. Explore the iconic Feilai Temple, hike up the majestic Seven-Star Crags, or unwind in the rejuvenating hot springs of Yingde - Qingyuan has something for every type of traveler. Immerse yourself in the local cuisine, wander through traditional villages, and create unforgettable memories in this off-the-beaten-path destination that will surely leave you in awe.
