About Wenzhou

Nestled between picturesque mountains and a stunning coastline, Wenzhou's charm lies in its juxtaposition of tradition and modernity. Wander through ancient temples and bustling markets in the morning, then dive into the vibrant nightlife scene in the evening. With a dynamic food culture that boasts local delicacies like stinky tofu and seafood hotpot, your taste buds are in for a treat. Explore the winding streets lined with traditional architecture, and don't miss the chance to haggle for souvenirs at one of the city's many shopping districts. Whether you're seeking cultural immersion or culinary adventures, Wenzhou offers a delightful mix of experiences that will leave you craving more.
