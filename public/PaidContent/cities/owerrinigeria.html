About Owerri

Owerri is a vibrant city bursting with energy, music, and color, making it a must-visit destination in Nigeria. From exploring the impressive Mbari Cultural and Art Centre to indulging in the mouthwatering local cuisine at the bustling markets, Owerri offers a true taste of Nigerian culture. Don't miss the chance to experience the lively nightlife scene, where you can dance to the beat of afrobeats and highlife music. With its friendly locals and fascinating mix of tradition and modernity, Owerri promises an unforgettable and authentically Nigerian adventure.
