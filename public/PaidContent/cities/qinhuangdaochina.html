About Qinhuangdao

Nestled along the Bohai Sea, Qinhuangdao in China embodies a unique blend of historical richness and seaside charm. From exploring the iconic Great Wall's eastern end at Shanhaiguan Pass to strolling along the tranquil beaches of Beidaihe, this coastal gem offers a delightful mix of cultural immersion and relaxation. Indulge in fresh seafood delights at the bustling Haibin Road Food Street or witness the spectacular sunrise over the sea at Laohushi Beach. Qinhuangdao seamlessly marries tradition with a modern flair, making it a must-visit destination for travelers seeking a balance of culture and seaside serenity.
