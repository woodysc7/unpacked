About Nanchang

Nanchang, nestled along the Gan River, effortlessly combines ancient history with modern charm, making it a gem waiting to be discovered in eastern China. Wander through the bustling streets to uncover hidden temples and traditional teahouses, or escape the urban buzz at the serene Tengwang Pavilion. Indulge in local delicacies like savory rice noodles and fresh seafood at the vibrant markets, then dance the night away in lively bars along Bayi Avenue. With its captivating mix of old and new, Nanchang is a city that promises to surprise and delight at every turn.
