About Homs

Homs, Syria, a city rich in history and resilience, offers a unique blend of ancient ruins and modern life. Wander through the narrow streets of the Old City, where you can explore the iconic Khalid ibn al-Walid Mosque and the majestic Citadel of Homs. Indulge in the vibrant flavors of local cuisine at bustling markets or sip sweet Arabic coffee in cozy cafes. Despite its tumultuous past, Homs welcomes visitors with warm hospitality and a spirit of hope for the future, making it a destination that truly captivates the soul.
