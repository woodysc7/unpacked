About Ordos City

Welcome to Ordos City, a fascinating destination filled with cultural wonders and modern marvels! Explore the vast Kubuqi Desert on camelback, wander through the unique Genghis Khan Mausoleum, and be amazed by the futuristic Kangbashi District. Enjoy traditional Mongolian cuisine, shop for local handicrafts at the bustling markets, and witness the vibrant blend of ancient traditions and contemporary lifestyles in this hidden gem of Inner Mongolia.Ordos City promises a truly unforgettable adventure for every curious traveler seeking a glimpse into China's diverse and dynamic landscape.
