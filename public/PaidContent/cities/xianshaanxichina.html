About Xi-an Shaanxi

Xi'an, located in the vibrant province of Shaanxi, China, is a fascinating mix of ancient history and modern charm. Best known for the awe-inspiring Terracotta Army, this city also offers bustling markets, mouthwatering street food, and a lively Muslim Quarter. Stroll along the ancient city walls, marvel at the Big Wild Goose Pagoda, and immerse yourself in the rich culture and heritage that permeates every corner of Xi'an. Whether you're a history buff, a foodie, or an adventurer seeking new experiences, Xi'an has something unique and unforgettable to offer every traveler.
