About Xintai

Xintai, China may not be on everyone's travel radar yet, but this hidden gem offers a delightful mix of ancient charm and modern allure. Explore the bustling night markets filled with tantalizing street food, visit the serene temples nestled among lush greenery, and take a leisurely stroll along the picturesque riverbanks. The locals are friendly, the food is delicious, and the vibrant energy of the city is contagious. Whether you're seeking cultural immersion or simply looking for a new adventure off the beaten path, Xintai is a destination that promises to surprise and enchant even the most seasoned travelers.
