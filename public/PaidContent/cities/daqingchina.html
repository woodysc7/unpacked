About Daqing

Daqing, China, may not be on everyone’s travel radar, but this bustling city in Heilongjiang Province is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered. Known for its rich history in oil production, Daqing offers visitors a unique blend of modernity and tradition. Explore the fascinating Petroleum Museum, stroll through the picturesque People's Park, and indulge in delicious local cuisine at the vibrant night markets. With its welcoming locals and vibrant energy, Daqing promises an authentic Chinese experience off the beaten path. Go ahead, immerse yourself in the charm of Daqing – you won't be disappointed.
