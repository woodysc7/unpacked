About Vellore

Vellore, a city in southern India, is a hidden gem waiting to be explored. Steeped in history and culture, it boasts a magnificent 16th-century fort that will transport you back in time. Wander through bustling bazaars, savoring flavors of authentic South Indian cuisine, and immerse yourself in the vibrant local life. Don't miss the chance to visit the renowned Christian Medical College, a pioneering institution in healthcare. Whether you're a history buff, a foodie, or simply seeking an off-the-beaten-path adventure, Vellore will captivate you with its charm and warmth.
