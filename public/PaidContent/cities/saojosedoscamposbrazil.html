About Sao Jose dos Campos

Nestled in the heart of Brazil's bustling state of São Paulo, Sao Jose dos Campos offers a delightful blend of nature, culture, and innovation. Known as the "Aerospace Capital of Brazil," this city boasts a fascinating mix of modern technology and historical charm. Explore the lush Parque Roberto Burle Marx, where vibrant flora and fauna provide the perfect backdrop for a leisurely stroll. Don't miss the stunning Basilica of Our Lady of Aparecida, a sacred site revered by pilgrims and history buffs alike. With its eclectic dining scene, vibrant markets, and welcoming locals, Sao Jose dos Campos beckons travelers to experience its unique fusion of past and present.
