About Quzhou

Nestled in the heart of Zhejiang province, Quzhou is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered by intrepid travelers seeking an authentic Chinese experience off the beaten path. Boasting a rich historical heritage dating back over 1800 years, this charming city offers a delightful mix of ancient temples, serene bamboo forests, and traditional tea plantations. Explore the picturesque landscapes of Jianglang Mountain, marvel at the intricate carvings of Longyou Caves, and immerse yourself in the local culture through vibrant night markets and authentic culinary delights. Quzhou may not be on every tourist's radar, but those who venture here are sure to be rewarded with unforgettable memories and a true taste of old-world China.
