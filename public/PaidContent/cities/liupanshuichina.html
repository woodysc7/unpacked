About Liupanshui

Welcome to Liupanshui, China, where vibrant culture and stunning natural beauty collide! Nestled in the heart of Guizhou province, this charming city offers a perfect blend of traditional Chinese charm and modern amenities. Immerse yourself in the local culture as you explore ancient temples, bustling markets, and indulge in delicious street food. For the outdoor enthusiasts, don't miss the breathtaking karst landscapes of Zhongshan Park or take a leisurely boat ride along the tranquil Hongfeng Lake. Whether you're a history buff, nature lover, or foodie at heart, Liupanshui has something special to offer every traveler.
