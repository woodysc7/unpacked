About Amman

Amman, the vibrant capital of Jordan, is a bustling metropolis where ancient history meets modern charm. Lose yourself in the labyrinthine streets of the old town, where colorful markets burst with spices and textiles. Climb to the hilltop Citadel for panoramic views of the cityscape and marvel at the Roman Amphitheater nestled in the heart of downtown. Indulge in delectable mezze dishes at local eateries and sip sweet mint tea like a true Jordanian. Whether exploring the enchanting souks, wandering through the art galleries, or simply soaking in the warmth of its hospitable people, Amman will capture your heart and ignite your sense of adventure.
