About Leshan

Leshan, China is a charming city brimming with history, culture, and natural beauty. Home to the awe-inspiring Leshan Giant Buddha, carved into a cliff face over a thousand years ago, this iconic landmark is a sight to behold. Visitors can stroll through the ancient streets, sample delicious Sichuan cuisine, and soak in the tranquil ambiance along the riverside. Don't miss the opportunity to take a boat ride to get a unique view of the majestic Buddha from the water. With its mix of old-world charm and modern amenities, Leshan offers a delightful blend of experiences for travelers seeking a memorable and culturally rich adventure.
