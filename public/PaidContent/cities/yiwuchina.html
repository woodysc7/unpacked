About Yiwu

Yiwu, China, often referred to as the "World's Supermarket," is a treasure trove for bargain hunters and souvenir seekers. This bustling city boasts the largest small commodities wholesale market in the world, offering everything from unique trinkets to bulk goods at unbeatable prices. But beyond the shopping extravaganza, Yiwu also surprises visitors with its rich cultural heritage, picturesque temples, and delicious local cuisine, making it a delightful destination for an authentic Chinese experience. Get lost in the labyrinthine alleys of the market, savor mouthwatering street food, and immerse yourself in the vibrant energy of this charming city.
