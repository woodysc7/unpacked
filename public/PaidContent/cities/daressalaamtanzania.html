About Dar es Salaam

Dar es Salaam, Tanzania, is a vibrant blend of old-world charm and modern city life. From the bustling Kariakoo Market to the tranquil shores of Coco Beach, this coastal city offers a taste of authentic East African culture. Explore the historic landmarks of the National Museum and Village Museum, or indulge in the diverse culinary scene with a plate of fresh seafood at the waterfront restaurants. With its lively street markets, colorful taxis, and friendly locals always ready with a warm "karibu," Dar es Salaam is a city that will captivate your senses and leave you longing for more.
