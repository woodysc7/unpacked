About Xiamen

Nestled on the southeast coast of China, Xiamen effortlessly blends tradition with modernity to create a vibrant and captivating destination for travelers. From strolling along the charming pedestrian-only lanes of Gulangyu Island to indulging in the city's renowned seafood delicacies at the bustling Zhongshan Road, Xiamen offers a perfect mix of cultural experiences and culinary delights. Don't miss the chance to admire the panoramic views of the city from Sunlight Rock or relax on the pristine beaches of Baicheng. Xiamen's warm climate, friendly locals, and intriguing historical sites make it a must-visit destination for anyone seeking a unique Chinese adventure.
