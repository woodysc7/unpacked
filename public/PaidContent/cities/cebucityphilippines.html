About Cebu City

Welcome to Cebu City, where the hustle and bustle of a metropolis meets the vibrant spirit of Filipino culture. Dive into the chaos of Carbon Market, bargain for unique souvenirs at Taboan Market, or indulge in a mouthwatering lechon feast. Take a leisurely stroll along the historic streets of Colon, the oldest in the country, and marvel at the grandeur of Magellan's Cross and the Basilica Minore del Santo Niño. For a dash of adventure, hop on a boat to the neighboring islands for some world-class diving or snorkeling. With friendly locals and flavors that will tantalize your taste buds, Cebu City is a lively destination that will capture your heart.
