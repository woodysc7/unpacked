About Cheongju

Cheongju, a vibrant city in South Korea, effortlessly blends tradition with modernity, offering visitors a unique and diverse travel experience. From exploring the historic streets of Hanok Village to indulging in the local culinary delights at the bustling street markets, there is something for everyone in Cheongju. Make sure to visit the Cheongju Craft Biennale, a celebration of creativity and craftsmanship that showcases the city's artistic soul. With its charming mix of old and new, Cheongju is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered by intrepid travelers seeking authenticity and cultural richness in their journey.
