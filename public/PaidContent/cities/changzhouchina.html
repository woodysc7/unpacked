About Changzhou

Nestled in the heart of Jiangsu province, Changzhou is a vibrant tapestry of ancient traditions and modern innovation. From the historic charm of Tianning Temple to the adrenaline rush of the thrilling roller coasters at China Dinosaur Park, this city offers a diverse array of experiences for travelers seeking both cultural immersion and exciting adventures. Don't miss the chance to sample the local culinary delights, like the famous Changzhou Wontons or savory Changzhou-style fried rice. Whether you're exploring the picturesque canals of the old town or marveling at the futuristic skyline, Changzhou is a captivating destination that seamlessly blends the past with the present.
