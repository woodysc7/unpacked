About Xingtai

Nestled in Hebei province, Xingtai is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered by intrepid travelers. This vibrant city seamlessly blends ancient history with modern charm, offering a glimpse into traditional Chinese culture amidst bustling urban streets. From exploring the ancient Longxing Temple to savoring delectable local cuisine at bustling food markets, Xingtai promises a unique, authentic experience off the beaten path. With friendly locals eager to share their stories and breathtaking natural landscapes waiting to be explored, Xingtai is a delightful surprise for those seeking a genuine taste of China beyond the usual tourist hotspots.
