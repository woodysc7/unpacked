About Malappuram

Nestled in the lush greenery of Kerala, Malappuram is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered. With its rolling hills, picturesque landscapes, and warm, welcoming locals, this vibrant town offers a taste of authentic South Indian culture. From exploring ancient temples and mosques to savoring spicy street food delights, Malappuram will charm you with its rich history and mouthwatering cuisine. Soak in the tranquility of the nearby beaches or embark on a thrilling wildlife safari in the surrounding forests – whatever your heart desires, Malappuram has it all. Experience the magic of this off-the-beaten-path destination and create unforgettable memories in the heart of God's Own Country.
