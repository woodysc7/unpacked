About Jinzhong

Nestled in the heart of Shanxi province, Jinzhong is a hidden gem that combines ancient charm with modern allure. Roam through the narrow alleyways of the old town, where traditional courtyard homes whisper stories of dynasties past. Take a leisurely stroll around Wang Family Courtyard, a sprawling complex that showcases exquisite Qing-style architecture. Dive into the vibrant local food scene and savor savory Shanxi noodles and mouthwatering steamed buns. With its rich history, picturesque landscapes, and warm hospitality, Jinzhong invites you to uncover the essence of authentic China in a place where the past and present harmoniously intertwine.
