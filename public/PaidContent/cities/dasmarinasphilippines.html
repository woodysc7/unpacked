About Dasmarinas

Nestled in the heart of Cavite, Dasmarinas effortlessly blends old-world charm with modern allure. From vibrant markets teeming with local treasures to lush parks perfect for a leisurely stroll, this city offers a delightful mix of experiences for every type of traveler. Embark on a culinary adventure and savor the bold flavors of Filipino cuisine or immerse yourself in the rich history at the Aguinaldo Shrine. Whether you're seeking bustling city life or a tranquil escape, Dasmarinas welcomes you with open arms and promises a memorable journey that will leave you craving more.
