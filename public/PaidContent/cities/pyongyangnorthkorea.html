About Pyongyang

Pyongyang, the secretive capital of North Korea, offers travelers a fascinating yet tightly controlled peek into the enigmatic world of the Hermit Kingdom. From the imposing Juche Tower to the grand Mansu Hill Grand Monument, the city is a showcase of state propaganda and meticulous urban planning. Visitors can marvel at the striking architecture of the Ryugyong Hotel or take a surreal stroll along the eerily empty streets. While the city's strict rules and limited interaction with locals can be challenging, experiencing the uniquely preserved time capsule of Pyongyang is an unforgettable adventure for the truly intrepid traveler.
