About Karaj

Karaj, Iran, is a vibrant city nestled in the Alborz Mountains, offering a delightful mix of modernity and tradition. Stroll through the bustling bazaars filled with colorful spices and handmade crafts, or hike to the top of Tochal Mountain for breathtaking views. Indulge in delicious kebabs at local eateries, explore the beautiful Bagh-e Ferdows Garden, or unwind in the relaxing Hammam-e Sultan Amir Ahmad bathhouse. With its warm hospitality and rich cultural heritage, Karaj is a hidden gem just waiting to be discovered.
