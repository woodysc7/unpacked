About Huaian

Welcome to Huaian, a charming city in eastern China known for its rich history, delicious cuisine, and picturesque landscapes. Take a leisurely stroll along the Qinhuai River, lined with traditional buildings and blooming willow trees, or explore the ancient architectural marvels such as the Chuxiu Garden and the Tieshan Temple. Don't miss the chance to savor local specialties like steamed buns and sesame pancakes at bustling street markets. Whether you're a history buff, a foodie, or simply seeking a tranquil escape, Huaian offers something for every traveler to enjoy.
