About Alexandria

Alexandria, Egypt, where ancient history meets vibrant modernity in a city bursting with energy and charm. From the iconic Bibliotheca Alexandrina to the bustling streets of the Montazah Palace district, this coastal gem offers a sensory feast for travelers seeking a blend of cultural immersion and relaxation. Stroll along the Corniche at sunset, savoring the salty breeze and the tantalizing aromas of street food vendors, or delve into the city's rich past at the Catacombs of Kom el Shoqafa and Pompey's Pillar. With its captivating mix of Mediterranean allure and historical wonders, Alexandria beckons adventurers to uncover its treasures and create memories that will last a lifetime.
