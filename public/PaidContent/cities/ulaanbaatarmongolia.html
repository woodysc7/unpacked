About Ulaanbaatar

Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia, is a vibrant city pulsating with a unique mix of ancient traditions and modern influences. From the bustling streets filled with nomadic herders in traditional attire to the contemporary cafes serving up local delicacies like khuushuur, every corner reveals a captivating juxtaposition of old and new. Explore the iconic Gandantegchinlen Monastery, delve into the rich history at the National Museum of Mongolia, or embark on a nomadic homestay in the vast Mongolian steppe for a truly immersive experience. Whether you're admiring the intricate artwork in Choijin Lama Temple Museum or savoring the flavors of a hearty buuz dumpling, Ulaanbaatar promises an adventure like no other.
