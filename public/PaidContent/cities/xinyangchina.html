About Xinyang

Escape the crowds and immerse yourself in authentic Chinese culture in the charming city of Xinyang. Wander through bustling markets filled with exotic fruits and local delicacies, or sip tea in serene traditional teahouses hidden down labyrinthine alleyways. Explore ancient temples, where the scent of incense mingles with the sound of chanting monks. For a taste of adventure, hike up the lush Luoshan Mountains for panoramic views of the city below. With its rich history, delicious cuisine, and warm hospitality, Xinyang is a hidden gem just waiting to be discovered.
