About Puyang

Tucked away in the eastern part of Henan province, Puyang is a hidden gem waiting to be explored. Beyond its bustling city center lies a tapestry of ancient wonders, from the majestic Puzhao Temple to the mysterious Dragon Valley. Dive into the local cuisine scene, where spicy noodles and crispy dumplings tantalize your taste buds. For an adrenaline rush, hop on a hot air balloon and soar over the enchanting Puyang landscape. Whether you're a history buff, a foodie, or an adventure seeker, Puyang offers a memorable and authentic Chinese experience that will leave you craving more.
