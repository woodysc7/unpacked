About Leiyang

Nestled in the heart of Hunan province, Leiyang may not be as well-known as its neighbors, but this hidden gem is a treat waiting to be discovered. Wander through bustling local markets overflowing with vibrant fruits and vegetables, tantalize your taste buds with spicy Hunan cuisine at hole-in-the-wall eateries, and hike up the scenic Tianmen Mountain for breathtaking views of the surrounding countryside. Immerse yourself in the rich history and culture of this charming city, and you'll quickly find yourself falling in love with the warmth of its people and the authenticity of its experiences. Leiyang may be off the beaten path, but it's a destination that promises surprises and delights at every turn.
