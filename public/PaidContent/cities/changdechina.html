About Changde

Nestled in the heart of Hunan province, Changde is a hidden gem waiting to be explored. From the captivating sights of the Jinjiang River winding through the city to the bustling markets offering a feast for the senses, Changde offers a blend of traditional charm and modern vibrancy. Take a leisurely stroll through the ancient streets of Wulingyuan or challenge your taste buds with the spicy local delicacies. For a truly immersive experience, climb the towering peaks of the nearby Zhangjiajie National Forest Park and let the breathtaking views leave you in awe. Changde may not be on everyone's radar, but those who venture here are rewarded with an unforgettable adventure in the heart of China.
