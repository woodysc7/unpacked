About Banjarmasin

Banjarmasin, affectionately known as the" Venice of the East," offers a unique and vibrant experience for travelers seeking authenticity. Navigate the bustling rivers on colorful water taxis, be enchanted by the mesmerizing floating markets where locals trade goods from the comfort of their boats, and savor the rich flavors of traditional Banjarese cuisine. Immerse yourself in the dynamic culture by exploring ornate mosques, wandering through lively night markets, and witnessing the breathtaking spectacle of thousands of flickering lanterns during the charming Banjarmasin Festival of Lights. Let Banjarmasin capture your heart with its warm hospitality and enchanting blend of tradition and modernity.
