About Cherthala

Cherthala, a charming town in the southern state of Kerala, India, offers a delightful mix of tranquil backwaters, lush greenery, and vibrant local culture. Wander through the bustling marketplaces where you can sample delicious local snacks like banana chips and fresh seafood. Explore the intricately designed temples and churches, each telling a story of the town's rich history and architectural heritage. For a truly unique experience, hop on a houseboat and cruise through the serene backwaters, witnessing everyday life unfold along the picturesque waterways. Cherthala is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered, promising a perfect blend of relaxation and cultural exploration for any savvy traveler.
