About Bacolod

Welcome to Bacolod, the City of Smiles! Known for its vibrant MassKara Festival, this charming city exudes warmth and hospitality that will make you feel right at home. Indulge in delicious local delicacies such as the famous chicken inasal, satisfy your sweet tooth with mouthwatering piaya and try the refreshing calamansi juice. Explore the heritage sites like The Ruins and immerse yourself in the rich history and culture of this bustling city. Whether you're here to feast on delicious food, dance to lively music, or simply soak in the friendly atmosphere, Bacolod is sure to leave you grinning from ear to ear.
