About Baoji

Nestled in the heart of Shaanxi province, Baoji is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered by intrepid travelers seeking an authentic taste of China. With its vibrant street markets, ancient temples, and mouthwatering local cuisine, Baoji offers a refreshing escape from the hustle and bustle of larger cities. Don't miss the chance to hike up the majestic Taibai Mountain, soak in the rejuvenating hot springs, or savor a steaming bowl of hand-pulled noodles at a cozy noodle shop. Whether you're a history buff, a foodie, or a nature enthusiast, Baoji has something special in store for everyone willing to explore its charming streets and welcoming community.
