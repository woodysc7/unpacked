About Tiruppur

Tiruppur, a vibrant city in the southern state of Tamil Nadu, is a hidden gem awaiting discovery. Known as the "Knitwear Capital of India," this bustling hub offers a unique blend of traditional culture and modern innovation. Explore the colorful markets brimming with exquisite textiles, sample mouthwatering local delicacies, and immerse yourself in the rich heritage of the region. From majestic temples to scenic gardens, Tiruppur has something for every traveler seeking an authentic Indian experience. Come for the textiles, stay for the charm—Tiruppur is a destination that will capture your heart and leave you wanting more.
