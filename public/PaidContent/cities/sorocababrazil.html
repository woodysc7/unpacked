About Sorocaba

Sorocaba, Brazil, a hidden gem nestled in the heart of São Paulo state, offers a delightful mix of vibrant culture, historical charm, and natural beauty. Stroll through the cobblestone streets of the Old Town, where colorful colonial buildings stand in contrast to modern street art, showcasing the city's dynamic spirit. Indulge in the local cuisine at bustling markets, savoring traditional dishes like pão de queijo and feijoada. For nature lovers, the city's lush parks and botanical gardens provide a peaceful escape from the urban buzz. Whether you're exploring museums, shopping at quaint boutiques, or simply soaking in the lively atmosphere of the bustling plazas, Sorocaba is sure to captivate you with its authentic Brazilian flair.
