About Meishan

Meishan, nestled in the heart of Sichuan province, is a hidden gem waiting to be explored. Forget the bustling crowds of Chengdu, Meishan offers a quintessential taste of authentic Chinese culture without the tourist traps. Wander through ancient temples, savor the fiery flavors of Sichuan cuisine, and lose yourself in the tranquil beauty of the surrounding countryside. Whether you're seeking a peaceful escape or a culinary adventure, Meishan promises an unforgettable journey off the beaten path.
