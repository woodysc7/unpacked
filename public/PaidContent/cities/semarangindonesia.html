About Semarang

Nestled along the northern coast of Java, Semarang enchants visitors with its delightful mix of old-world charm and modern bustle. Wander through the charming streets of the Old Town, where colonial-era architecture stands testament to its rich history, or dive into the vibrant chaos of Chinatown for a sensory overload of sights, sounds, and smells. Don't miss climbing up to the iconic Sam Poo Kong temple for panoramic city views or indulging in the city's culinary delights, from street-side satay to mouthwatering lumpia. Whether you're exploring its cultural heritage or savoring its culinary delights, Semarang promises a memorable adventure for every traveler.
