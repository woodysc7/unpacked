About Kolhapur

Welcome to Kolhapur, a vibrant city in Maharashtra, India, known for its rich history, delectable cuisine, and distinct cultural heritage. Explore the magnificent Kolhapur Mahalaxmi Temple, a sacred site dedicated to the goddess Mahalaxmi, and witness traditional performances of Lavani dance that will leave you mesmerized. Indulge in the local specialty, the spicy and flavorful Kolhapuri cuisine, and savor the famous Kolhapuri misal, a dish that packs a punch. Don't miss out on shopping for intricately designed Kolhapuri chappals, handcrafted leather sandals that are a perfect souvenir to take back home. Kolhapur welcomes you with open arms to experience its warmth, beauty, and charm.
