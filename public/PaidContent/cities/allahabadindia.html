About Allahabad

Allahabad, located at the confluence of the Ganges and Yamuna rivers in India, is a vibrant city steeped in history and culture. From the grandeur of the Kumbh Mela festival held every 12 years to the serene beauty of the Triveni Sangam, where the two rivers meet, Allahabad offers a unique blend of spirituality and tradition. Explore the grand architecture of the Allahabad Fort, indulge in delicious street food at bustling markets, and witness the colorful chaos of everyday life in this enchanting city. Whether you're a history buff, a foodie, or a spiritual seeker, Allahabad has something for everyone to discover and enjoy.
