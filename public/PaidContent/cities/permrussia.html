About Perm

Nestled at the foothills of the Ural Mountains, Perm charms visitors with its unique blend of industrial grit and artistic flair. From the imposing Perm-36 Gulag Museum to the quirky Street Art Gallery, this city surprises at every turn. Dive into the local culture at the bustling Gorky Park or escape to the tranquil Perm Opera and Ballet Theatre for a dose of high culture. With its vibrant nightlife, hearty Russian cuisine, and welcoming locals, Perm is a destination that will leave you intrigued and inspired.
