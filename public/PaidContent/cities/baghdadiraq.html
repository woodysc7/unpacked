About Baghdad

Discover the vibrant tapestry of Baghdad, where ancient history meets bustling modernity in a city teeming with contrasts. Be captivated by the grandeur of the iconic Al-Mustansiriya University, immerse yourself in the lively chaos of Al-Shorja market, and wander through the serene beauty of Zawra Park. Indulge your taste buds with aromatic Iraqi cuisine, explore the rich heritage of the National Museum, and marvel at the intricate tile work of the stunning mosques. Baghdad is a city that will challenge your perceptions, ignite your senses, and leave you with a profound appreciation for its resilient spirit and warm hospitality.
