About Diyarbakir

Diyarbakir, a hidden gem tucked away in southeastern Turkey, is a mesmerizing blend of ancient history and vibrant culture waiting to be discovered. Stroll through the maze-like streets of the UNESCO-listed old town, where towering city walls whisper tales of empires past. Indulge in traditional Kurdish cuisine at bustling bazaars, savoring aromatic spices and hearty dishes. Immerse yourself in the lively atmosphere of the city's tea gardens, where locals gather to chat and sip çay. With its rich heritage, warm hospitality, and tantalizing flavors, Diyarbakir promises an unforgettable journey off the beaten path.
