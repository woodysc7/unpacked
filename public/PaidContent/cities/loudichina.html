About Loudi

Nestled in the heart of Hunan Province, Loudi surprises visitors with its vibrant charm and rich cultural heritage. From exploring lush tea plantations to savoring authentic spicy Hunan cuisine, this bustling city offers a true taste of local life. Dive into the bustling night markets, where the aroma of sizzling street food fills the air, or wander through the ancient temples that whisper tales of centuries past. Whether you're seeking adventure in the surrounding mountains or simply soaking in the lively atmosphere of the city streets, Loudi's warmth and welcoming spirit will leave you captivated long after you leave.
