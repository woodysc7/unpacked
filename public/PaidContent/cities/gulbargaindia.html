About Gulbarga

Gulbarga, a hidden gem in northern Karnataka, offers a delightful mix of history, culture, and warm hospitality. Wander through the ancient monuments of Gulbarga Fort, where echoes of the Bahmani Sultanate resonate through its imposing architecture. Indulge your taste buds with the local delicacies at the bustling markets, from spicy biryanis to delectable sweets. Take a peaceful moment at the serene Khwaja Bande Nawaz Dargah, a spiritual haven for seekers of all faiths. With its rich heritage and welcoming locals, Gulbarga promises a charming and authentic experience off the beaten path.
