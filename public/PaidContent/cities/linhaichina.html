About Linhai

With its ancient city walls, charming waterways, and rich history, Linhai in China's Zhejiang province is a hidden gem waiting to be explored. Wander through the narrow alleyways of the old town, marvel at the intricate wood carvings at Linhai Woodcarving Museum, and soak in the beauty of the scenic Qiantang River. Discover traditional tea houses, taste local delicacies, and immerse yourself in the vibrant culture of this picturesque coastal city. Whether you're a history buff, a nature lover, or a foodie, Linhai offers a delightful mix of experiences that will leave you enchanted and eager to explore more of China.
