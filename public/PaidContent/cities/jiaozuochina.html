About Jiaozuo

Nestled in the heart of Henan province, Jiaozuo is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered by intrepid travelers seeking an authentic Chinese experience. From the breathtaking Yuntai Mountain Scenic Area with its cascading waterfalls to the ancient ruins of the Yuntaishan Grottoes, this city offers a perfect blend of natural beauty and cultural heritage. The locals, known for their warm hospitality, will welcome you with open arms and introduce you to mouthwatering regional delicacies like spicy Jiaozuo hot pot. So go ahead and explore the charms of Jiaozuo – you won't be disappointed!
