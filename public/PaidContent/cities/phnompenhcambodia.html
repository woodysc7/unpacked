About Phnom Penh

Immerse yourself in the bustling streets and rich history of Phnom Penh, Cambodia's vibrant capital city. From the majestic Royal Palace to the somber yet important Killing Fields, this city offers a juxtaposition of beauty and tragedy. Indulge in the aromatic flavors of Khmer cuisine at the local markets, or sip on a refreshing glass of sugar cane juice as you navigate the chaotic traffic on a tuk-tuk. Whether you're exploring ancient temples, bargaining at the bustling Russian Market, or reflecting on the country's tumultuous past at the Tuol Sleng Genocide Museum, Phnom Penh will captivate your senses and leave you with a deeper understanding of Cambodia's complex and fascinating culture.
