About Kurnool

Nestled in the heart of Andhra Pradesh, Kurnool is a vibrant city bursting with history, culture, and flavors waiting to be explored. From the majestic Konda Reddy Fort offering stunning panoramic views to the tranquil Belum Caves, one of the largest underground cave systems in the world, Kurnool is a treasure trove of hidden gems. Indulge your taste buds with the local delicacies like Kandhi Podi or take a leisurely stroll along the Tungabhadra River to soak in the beauty of this bustling city. With warm hospitality and a rich tapestry of experiences, Kurnool invites you to uncover its magic and create unforgettable memories.
