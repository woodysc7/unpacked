Santo Domingo
Santo Domingo, the vibrant heart of the Dominican Republic, is a melting pot of history, culture, and Caribbean spirit. Get lost in the cobblestone streets of the Colonial Zone, where centuries-old buildings echo stories of conquest and resilience. Indulge in the lively rhythms of merengue and bachata that fill the night air, while savoring a delicious plate of mofongo. Immerse yourself in the art scene, with colorful murals adorning every corner, or escape to the nearby beaches for some sun-soaked relaxation. In Santo Domingo, every moment is an adventure waiting to be embraced.
