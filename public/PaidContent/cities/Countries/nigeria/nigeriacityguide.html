Lagos
Lagos, Nigeria, a bustling metropolis where vibrant energy meets rich culture, offers a sensory overload like no other. From the chaotic markets of Balogun to the serene beaches of Lekki, this city is a kaleidoscope of experiences waiting to be explored. Indulge in the mouth-watering jollof rice, sway to the infectious beats of Afrobeat music, and immerse yourself in the captivating history at the National Museum. And if you're feeling adventurous, navigate the infamous traffic like a true Lagosian on a yellow danfo bus. A trip to Lagos promises chaos, charm, and a whole lot of fun!
