About Hiroshima

Hiroshima, a city of immense resilience and vibrant culture, seamlessly weaves its tragic past with a hopeful future. Visitors are drawn to the poignant Peace Memorial Park, a sobering reminder of the atomic bombing in 1945, followed by the rejuvenated Hiroshima Castle and its surrounding cherry blossom gardens. The bustling Hondori shopping arcade offers a taste of modern Japan, while the tranquil island of Miyajima beckons with its iconic floating torii gate and friendly deer. Embrace the harmony of old and new in Hiroshima, a city that embodies the spirit of peace and progress.
