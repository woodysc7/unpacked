About Enugu

Enugu, Nigeria is a vibrant city that seamlessly marries tradition with modernity. From the bustling markets offering a sensory overload of sights, smells, and sounds, to the serene Awhum Waterfall that invites visitors to bask in nature's beauty, Enugu caters to a diverse range of interests. Sample the local delicacies like Nsala soup and Okpa at roadside eateries, or immerse yourself in the city's rich cultural heritage at the National Museum. With friendly locals eager to share their stories and a lively arts scene that pulses through the city, Enugu promises an authentic Nigerian experience that is as warm and inviting as its people.
