About Asuncion

Explore the charming hidden gem of Asuncion, Paraguay, where colonial architecture meets vibrant street art in a fusion of old and new. Dive into the local culture through lively street markets, where the aroma of traditional Paraguayan cuisine fills the air. Stroll along the scenic riverfront, where you can relax in shady parks and watch the sun set over the Paraguay River. Embrace the warm hospitality of the locals as you sip on a refreshing tereré, a traditional herbal tea. In Asuncion, you'll discover a city brimming with history, culture, and a unique South American spirit waiting to be uncovered.
