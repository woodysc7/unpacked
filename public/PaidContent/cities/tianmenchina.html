About Tianmen

Nestled in the heart of Zhangjiajie National Forest Park, Tianmen Mountain in China is a paradise for adventure seekers and nature lovers alike. The hair-raising cliffside glass walkway offers breathtaking views for the brave at heart, while the heart-pounding Tianmen Cave promises an exhilarating experience that will have you talking about it for years to come. A cable car ride to the mountain's summit treats visitors to panoramic vistas of the lush green landscape below, making Tianmen Mountain a must-visit destination for those seeking a mix of adrenaline and awe-inspiring beauty.
