About Samarinda

Nestled along the banks of the mighty Mahakam River, Samarinda in Indonesia offers a blend of bustling city life and serene natural beauty. Take a boat ride along the river to witness traditional stilt houses lining the shores, or head to the vibrant Pasar Pagi market to immerse yourself in the local culture. Indulge in fresh seafood delicacies at the floating restaurants or embark on a jungle trek to discover hidden waterfalls and exotic wildlife. With its warm hospitality and authentic Indonesian charm, Samarinda is a hidden gem waiting to be explored by adventurous travelers seeking an off-the-beaten-path experience.
