About Seregno

Nestled in the heart of the Lombardy region, Seregno is a charming Italian town waiting to be discovered. Wander through its picturesque streets lined with quaint cafes and gelaterias, where the scent of freshly baked bread and espresso fills the air. Don't miss the chance to explore the historic Villa Splendore, a stunning neoclassical villa surrounded by lush gardens, or indulge in the local culinary delights at the bustling food market. Whether you're sipping on aperitivo in a lively piazza or admiring the stunning architecture, Seregno offers a delightful taste of authentic Italian charm that will leave you coming back for more.
