About Luoyang

Luoyang, China, impresses with its fascinating blend of ancient history and modern charm. As the ancient capital of multiple dynasties, this city is a treasure trove of cultural wonders, from the magnificent Longmen Grottoes to the iconic White Horse Temple. Explore the bustling night markets for a taste of local delicacies and immerse yourself in the lively atmosphere of Luoyang Old Town. Whether you're a history buff, a foodie, or simply seeking an authentic Chinese experience, Luoyang offers a delightful mix of attractions that will leave you enchanted and craving for more.
