About Likasi

Welcome to Likasi, a vibrant city in the heart of the Democratic Republic of Congo! Known for its friendly locals, bustling markets, and rich mining history, Likasi offers a unique blend of culture and adventure. Explore the colorful streets filled with aromatic street food vendors, visit the breathtaking Kibwezi Caves, or immerse yourself in the local music and dance scene. Whether you're a history buff, a foodie, or an outdoor enthusiast, Likasi has something for everyone. So pack your bags and get ready to experience the energy and excitement of this hidden gem in the DRC!
