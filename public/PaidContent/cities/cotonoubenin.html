About Cotonou

Cotonou, Benin, is a vibrant city that pulsates with the rhythms of West African life. From the bustling Dantokpa Market where you can haggle for exotic spices and colorful fabrics, to the tranquil beauty of Fidjrossè Beach where you can unwind and watch the sunset over the Atlantic Ocean, Cotonou offers a diverse mix of experiences. Taste delicious street food like grilled fish and spicy peanut sauce, listen to the sounds of traditional voodoo drums, and explore the rich cultural heritage of this dynamic city. Whether you're a history buff, a foodie, or a beach lover, Cotonou has something for everyone to enjoy.
