About Xinmi

Experience the charming blend of ancient beauty and modern vibrancy in Xinmi, China. From exploring the historic Yinxu Ruins to wandering through the bustling Xinmi Old Town, there is no shortage of cultural immersion here. Indulge in local delicacies like steaming hot dumplings or sip on traditional tea while watching daily life unfold around you. With its friendly locals and serene landscapes, Xinmi offers a delightful escape from the ordinary, inviting you to uncover the hidden gems of this captivating city.
