About Chengdu

Welcome to Chengdu, the laid-back capital of Sichuan province in southwest China. Known as the panda capital of the world, Chengdu offers so much more than just adorable bears. Explore bustling street markets, sip on piping hot bowls of spicy Sichuan hotpot, and take leisurely strolls along the picturesque Jinli Ancient Street. Don't miss the chance to visit the UNESCO-listed Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding to get up close and personal with these lovable creatures. Chengdu's relaxed vibe, mouthwatering cuisine, and vibrant culture are sure to leave you wanting more.
