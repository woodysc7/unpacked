About Nyala

Step into the vibrant tapestry of Nyala, Sudan, where the bustling markets offer a sensory overload of colors and scents. As you navigate through the labyrinthine alleyways, you'll be greeted by friendly locals eager to share their culture and traditions. Don't miss the opportunity to savor a delicious plate of ful medames, a staple dish in Sudanese cuisine. For a dose of adventure, embark on a trek through the breathtaking Jebel Marra mountain range, where sweeping vistas and hidden waterfalls await. Nyala is a destination that will captivate your senses and leave you with lasting memories of its warmth and charm.
