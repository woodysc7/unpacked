About Medan

Medan, Indonesia, is a bustling city that offers a vibrant mix of culture, food, and history. From exploring the colorful markets filled with aromatic spices and fresh fruits to indulging in the city's famous culinary scene, where savory local dishes like rendang and soto are a must-try, Medan is a feast for the senses. Dive into the past at Maimun Palace, a stunning architectural gem, or take a trip to the serene Lake Toba for a relaxing retreat surrounded by lush greenery. With its warm hospitality and lively atmosphere, Medan is a destination that will leave you craving for more.
