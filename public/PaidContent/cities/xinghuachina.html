About Xinghua

Escape the bustling city life and immerse yourself in the serene beauty of Xinghua, China. Wander through lush green tea plantations, savor authentic local cuisine bursting with flavors, and explore hidden temples steeped in centuries of history. Feel the warmth of the welcoming locals and embrace the tranquil pace of life in this charming gem of a destination. Let Xinghua captivate you with its peaceful countryside vibes and leave you yearning for more of its simple yet extraordinary charm.
