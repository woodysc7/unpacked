About Shantou

Nestled along the eastern coast of Guangdong Province, Shantou is a vibrant and off-the-beaten-path gem waiting to be explored. From feasting on mouthwatering seafood at the bustling outdoor markets to strolling through the charming lanes of the old town lined with traditional Teochew architecture, this city offers a delightful mix of cultural experiences. Don't miss the chance to climb the picturesque Queshi Scenic Area for panoramic views of the city and the South China Sea. Whether you're a foodie, history buff, or nature lover, Shantou has something for everyone, making it a must-visit destination for those seeking an authentic taste of China beyond the usual tourist hotspots.
