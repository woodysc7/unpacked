About Liaoyang

Liaoyang, a hidden gem in northeastern China, offers a delightful blend of ancient history and modern charm. Wander through the well-preserved Liaoyang Ancient City, where you can immerse yourself in centuries-old architecture and picturesque gardens. Don't miss the striking White Pagoda, a symbol of the city's cultural heritage. For a taste of local life, explore the bustling Liaoyang Night Market and indulge in savory street food delights. With its rich history, vibrant culture, and warm hospitality, Liaoyang is a destination that promises a unique and unforgettable travel experience.
