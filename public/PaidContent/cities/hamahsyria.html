About Hamah

Located in northwestern Syria, Hamah is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered by intrepid travelers. With a history dating back to biblical times, this city is a captivating mix of ancient ruins, bustling markets, and charming cafes. The star attraction, the Norias of Hama, giant wooden water wheels that have been spinning for centuries, are a mesmerizing sight to behold. Stroll through the narrow alleys of the old town, sample delicious Syrian cuisine, and immerse yourself in the warm hospitality of the locals. Despite its tumultuous past, Hamah remains a place of resilience and beauty, offering a unique and unforgettable travel experience.
