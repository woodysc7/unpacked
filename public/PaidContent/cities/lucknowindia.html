About Lucknow

Lucknow, India, is a city of regal charm and culinary delights that will captivate your senses. Known as the "City of Nawabs," Lucknow exudes an old-world charm with its magnificent architecture and vibrant culture. From exploring the historical marvels of Bara Imambara and Chota Imambara to savoring the aromatic flavors of traditional Awadhi cuisine, every corner of Lucknow offers a glimpse into its rich heritage. Get lost in the bustling markets, witness the art of chikankari embroidery, and indulge in the famous kebabs that will leave you craving for more. Lucknow is a treasure trove waiting to be explored, offering a perfect blend of history, culture, and gastronomic delights.
