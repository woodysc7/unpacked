About Tongling

Tongling, a charming city in eastern China, surprises visitors with its unique blend of tradition and modernity. As you wander through the bustling streets, you'll encounter ancient temples standing next to contemporary skyscrapers, showcasing the city's rich history and rapid development. Make sure to sip on some locally-produced green tea at one of the cozy teahouses and indulge in the mouthwatering local cuisine, known for its fresh river fish dishes. Whether you explore the picturesque Baiji Mountain Park or engage in a lively mahjong game with the friendly locals, Tongling promises an unforgettable cultural experience that will leave you craving more.
