About Ganzhou

Nestled in the heart of Jiangxi province, Ganzhou is a vibrant city steeped in history and culture, offering a delightful mix of ancient traditions and modern charm. From exploring the majestic cliffs of Mount Jinggang to strolling through the bustling streets of the old town, Ganzhou has something to captivate every traveler. Sample the local delicacies, such as the famous Gannan navel oranges and mouth-watering tofu dishes, or wander through the bustling markets to shop for traditional crafts and souvenirs. With its warm hospitality and rich heritage, Ganzhou invites you to embark on an unforgettable journey through hidden gems and authentic experiences.
