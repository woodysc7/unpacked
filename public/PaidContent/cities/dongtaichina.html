About Dongtai

Nestled in Jiangsu province, Dongtai is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered by intrepid travelers seeking an authentic Chinese experience. Stroll through bustling street markets overflowing with unique antiques, taste mouthwatering street food delicacies like tangyuan and xiaolongbao, and marvel at the intricate architecture of ancient temples and pagodas. The charming blend of traditional culture and modern influences makes Dongtai a must-visit destination for those looking to step off the beaten path and immerse themselves in the heart of China.
