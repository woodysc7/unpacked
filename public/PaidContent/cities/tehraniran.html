About Tehran

Discover the heartbeat of Iran in Tehran, where ancient history meets modern vibrancy. Lose yourself in the bustling bazaars of the Grand Bazaar, savor aromatic kebabs at local eateries, and marvel at the stunning architecture of Golestan Palace. Immerse yourself in the rich Persian culture by exploring the city's museums, art galleries, and traditional tea houses. Don't miss the chance to hike up Mount Tochal for panoramic views of the sprawling metropolis below. Tehran will captivate you with its warm hospitality, intriguing contrasts, and a tapestry of sights and sounds that will leave you spellbound.
