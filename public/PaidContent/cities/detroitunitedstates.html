About Detroit

Detroit, a city known for its resilience and creativity, offers visitors a vibrant mix of history, culture, and innovation. From the iconic Motown Museum to the stunning Detroit Institute of Arts, there is no shortage of artistic inspiration to be found. Explore the bustling Eastern Market, sample delicious local cuisine at Corktown's trendy eateries, or take a stroll along the scenic Riverfront. Whether you're a music lover, a history buff, or a foodie, Detroit has something for everyone to enjoy. So pack your bags and discover the heart and soul of the Motor City!
