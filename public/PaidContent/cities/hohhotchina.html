About Hohhot

Nestled in the heart of Inner Mongolia, Hohhot is a vibrant and culturally rich city waiting to be explored. From the awe-inspiring Gandantegchinlen Monastery to the bustling Da Zhao Temple, the city offers a fascinating blend of ancient traditions and modern development. Don't miss the chance to savor traditional Mongolian cuisine at the bustling night markets or embark on a thrilling desert safari in the nearby Kubuqi Desert. Whether you're soaking in the local culture or marveling at the natural beauty of the grasslands, Hohhot promises a unique and unforgettable travel experience that will leave you craving more.
