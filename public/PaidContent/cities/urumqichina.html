About Urumqi

Urumqi, the bustling capital of China's Xinjiang region, is a city where East meets West in a vibrant clash of cultures and flavors. From wandering through the bustling Grand Bazaar to hiking the picturesque Tian Shan mountains, there is no shortage of experiences to be had. Indulge in rich Uyghur cuisine, explore ancient Silk Road remnants, or simply marvel at the stunning mosques and palaces dotting the cityscape. Urumqi is a place where history, tradition, and modernity blend seamlessly, offering travelers a unique and unforgettable adventure in the heart of Central Asia.
