About Beirut

Beirut is a vibrant mosaic of ancient wonders and modern delights, where history and hedonism blend seamlessly against the backdrop of the glistening Mediterranean Sea. Stroll through the charming streets of Gemmayze and Mar Mikhael, lined with trendy cafes and street art, before immersing yourself in the rich tapestry of cultures at the bustling Souk el Tayeb. Indulge in delectable Lebanese cuisine at family-run eateries, then dance the night away in the buzzing nightlife scene. With its resilient spirit and warm hospitality, Beirut is a city that captivates the soul and leaves you craving more.
