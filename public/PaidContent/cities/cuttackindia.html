About Cuttack

Cuttack, a hidden gem in eastern India, offers a delightful mix of history, culture, and mouthwatering street food that is sure to tantalize your taste buds. Explore the ancient temples and bustling markets that give this city its vibrant charm, or hop on a boat ride along the scenic Mahanadi River for a unique perspective of the bustling cityscape. Be prepared to get lost in the labyrinthine lanes of the Silver City, where every corner holds a treasure trove of authentic experiences waiting to be discovered. Whether you're a history buff, a foodie, or simply seeking a new adventure, Cuttack promises a memorable and enriching travel experience.
