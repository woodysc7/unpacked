About Zigong

Nestled in Sichuan's lush greenery, Zigong offers a delightful blend of ancient charm and vibrant culture. History buffs will revel in the city's rich heritage as the former capital of the ancient Shu Kingdom, while foodies can indulge in savory Sichuan cuisine at bustling street markets. Don't miss the mesmerizing Zigong Dinosaur Museum, home to life-sized dinosaur skeletons and interactive exhibits that will transport you back millions of years. With its picturesque parks, warm locals, and fiery cuisine, Zigong is a hidden gem waiting to be explored!
