About Nantong

Nestled along the Yangtze River, Nantong effortlessly blends ancient charm with modern allure. Stroll through the bustling street markets, where the tantalizing aromas of street food beckon you to taste your way through local delicacies. Climb to the top of Langshan Mountain for panoramic views of the cityscape below, or immerse yourself in the rich history of the region at the Nantong Museum. Whether you're seeking cultural enlightenment, culinary adventures, or simply a picturesque riverside escape, Nantong offers a delightful blend of experiences that will leave you enchanted and eagerly planning your return trip.
