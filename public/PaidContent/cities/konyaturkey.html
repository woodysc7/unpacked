About Konya

Located in the heart of Turkey, Konya is a city that seamlessly weaves together tradition and modernity. Famous for its beautifully preserved Seljuk architecture, vibrant bazaars, and mouthwatering cuisine, Konya offers visitors a glimpse into Turkey's rich cultural tapestry. Don't miss the iconic Mevlana Museum, a tribute to the renowned Sufi poet Rumi, or the mesmerizing Whirling Dervishes ceremony that takes place here. Take a stroll through the historic streets, sample some delicious local dishes, and immerse yourself in the warm hospitality of the locals - Konya will capture your heart from the moment you arrive.
