Dominica
Dominica, known as the "Nature Island of the Caribbean," boasts lush rainforests, towering mountains, and pristine rivers waiting to be explored. Its rugged terrain is dotted with volcanic peaks, including the famous Boiling Lake and Morne Trois Pitons, a UNESCO World Heritage site. The island's diverse landscape offers opportunities for hiking, diving, and birdwatching, with colorful coral reefs and vibrant marine life just off its shores. Dominica's tropical climate ensures warm temperatures year-round, making it an ideal destination for outdoor enthusiasts looking to immerse themselves in nature's beauty.
