About Aurangabad

Aurangabad, a vibrant city steeped in history and culture, is a hidden gem waiting to be explored in India. Known as the" City of Gates," Aurangabad boasts stunning architectural wonders such as the iconic Ajanta and Ellora Caves, showcasing ancient rock-cut temples and intricate sculptures that will leave you in awe. Immerse yourself in the bustling markets, savor mouthwatering local cuisine, and mingle with the friendly locals who are eager to share their stories. Aurangabad is a delightful fusion of past and present, offering a unique and unforgettable travel experience for those seeking adventure off the beaten path.
