About Bulawayo

Bulawayo, Zimbabwe's second-largest city, is a vibrant cultural hub teeming with historical charm and warm hospitality. From exploring the impressive Khami Ruins and the captivating Natural History Museum to indulging in the local delicacies at the bustling Tshabalala Market, visitors are sure to be immersed in the authentic essence of Zimbabwean life. The city's laid-back pace allows for leisurely strolls through leafy parks and engaging conversations with friendly locals, making Bulawayo a truly unmissable destination for curious travelers seeking a genuine African experience.
