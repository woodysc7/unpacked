About Xining

Nestled in the heart of the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau, Xining offers a unique blend of cultural charm and natural beauty. From browsing the bustling Dongguan Mosque, one of the largest in China, to exploring the stunning Kumbum Monastery with its intricate Tibetan architecture, the city is a treasure trove for history buffs and architecture enthusiasts alike. For the adventurous souls, a journey to the otherworldly Qinghai Lake provides a breathtaking escape with its azure waters and snow-capped mountains in the backdrop. As you roam the lively streets filled with aromatic food stalls offering local delicacies like hand-pulled noodles and lamb skewers, you'll quickly fall in love with the vibrant energy and warm hospitality that Xining exudes, making it a truly unforgettable destination in Western China.
