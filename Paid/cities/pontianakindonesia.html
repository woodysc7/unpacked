About Pontianak

Pontianak, Indonesia, is not your typical tourist destination, but that's what makes it so intriguing. Known as the Equator City, Pontianak sits right on the line that divides the Earth's northern and southern hemispheres. Visitors can literally straddle two worlds by visiting the Equator Monument. The city's vibrant markets offer a sensory overload of exotic fruits, spices, and local handicrafts, while the bustling riverfront provides a glimpse into daily life in this unique river city. For those seeking an off-the-beaten-path adventure with a touch of equatorial flair, Pontianak is waiting to be discovered.
