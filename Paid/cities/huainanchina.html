About Huainan

Nestled in the heart of Anhui Province, Huainan is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered. This city effortlessly blends tradition with modernity, offering visitors a glimpse into authentic Chinese culture. Stroll through the bustling night markets, where the aroma of sizzling street food fills the air and bright lanterns illuminate your path. Don't miss a visit to the stunning Bagong Mountain, where ancient temples and lush forests await eager hikers. Whether you're a history buff exploring the city's rich past or a foodie indulging in local delicacies, Huainan will captivate you with its charm and warmth.
