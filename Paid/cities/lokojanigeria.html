About Lokoja

Lokoja, perched along the banks of the mighty Niger and Benue rivers, is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered in Nigeria. Steeped in history as the country's first capital, this charming city offers a vibrant mix of cultures, from the bustling local markets to the majestic colonial architecture lining its streets. Adventurers can embark on a river cruise to explore the scenic confluence where the rivers meet, or hike up Mount Patti for panoramic views of the city below. With its warm hospitality and rich heritage, Lokoja promises an enriching and unforgettable travel experience in the heart of Nigeria.
