About Datong

Datong, China, is a captivating mix of ancient wonders and modern charm. Stepping into the city feels like journeying back in time with its stunning Yungang Grottoes, a UNESCO World Heritage site boasting intricate Buddhist statues carved into caves dating back over 1,500 years. However, Datong also surprises visitors with its contemporary vibe seen in its vibrant street art scene and trendy cafes. Don't miss the chance to sample the local delicacies like Datong's famous roasted lamb skewers or explore the historic city walls that offer sweeping views of the surrounding area. Whether you're a history buff, foodie, or art enthusiast, Datong has something to offer every traveler seeking a unique and memorable experience.
