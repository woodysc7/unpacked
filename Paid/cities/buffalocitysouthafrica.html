About Buffalo City

Nestled along the scenic Sunshine Coast of South Africa, Buffalo City effortlessly marries natural beauty with vibrant urban energy. From the stunning beaches of East London to the lively boardwalk at Nahoon, this coastal gem offers an array of adventures for every traveler. Immerse yourself in the rich history at the East London Museum, indulge in fresh seafood delights at the local markets, or catch some waves at the world-renowned Nahoon Reef. Whether you're seeking relaxation or exhilarating experiences, Buffalo City beckons with its warm hospitality and endless possibilities.
