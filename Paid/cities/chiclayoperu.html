About Chiclayo

Chiclayo, a vibrant city on the northern coast of Peru, is a hidden gem for travelers seeking a mix of rich cultural heritage and sun-soaked beaches. Dive into the bustling Mercado Modelo, where the aroma of ceviche and exotic fruits fills the air, or explore the fascinating Royal Tombs of Sipán Museum to uncover ancient Moche artifacts. Don't miss the chance to soak up the sun on the sandy shores of Pimentel Beach, where you can sample fresh seafood and catch a colorful sunset. With its lively atmosphere and warm hospitality, Chiclayo will charm you from the moment you arrive.
