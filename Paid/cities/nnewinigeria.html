About Nnewi

Nnewi, Nigeria is a vibrant and bustling city that offers a unique and authentic taste of Igbo culture. Known as the "Japan of Africa" for its entrepreneurial spirit and thriving manufacturing industry, Nnewi will impress you with its entrepreneurial drive and innovation. Explore the colorful markets filled with traditional crafts and sample the delicious local cuisine, from spicy jollof rice to mouthwatering suya. Be sure to visit the Nnewi Motorcycle Market, where you can marvel at rows of shiny new bikes lined up for sale. Whether you're a business traveler or a curious tourist, Nnewi is a destination that will surprise and inspire you at every turn.
