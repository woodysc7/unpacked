Greenland
Greenland, the world's largest island, is a land of dramatic contrasts and stunning natural beauty. Its vast ice sheet covers about 80% of the island, with towering glaciers and icebergs dotting the coastline. The landscape is characterized by rugged mountains, deep fjords, and colorful villages nestled along the shores. The climate varies from region to region, with the southern coast experiencing milder temperatures and the interior being much colder. Travelers to Greenland can explore the unique Inuit culture, go hiking or skiing in the pristine wilderness, and witness the breathtaking beauty of the Northern Lights.
